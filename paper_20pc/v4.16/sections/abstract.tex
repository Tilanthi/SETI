\begin{abstract}
Searches for narrowband transmitters above about 30\,GHz have reached a single
spectral line toward a few nearby stars. Uniformly re-reduced, \NEB{} ALMA execution blocks toward
\NSysClassA{} systems give \NWinA{} fine-channel windows with
\ChanLoA--\ChanHiA\,kHz channels, covering \CvUnionA\,GHz of union bandwidth
in \CovNIslands{} disjoint intervals between \SurvFreqLoA{} and
\SurvFreqHiA\,GHz; \NWinB{} coarser windows carry no drift discrimination.
Windows are filtered for an unresolved carrier drifting linearly in the
stellar frame; crossings are tested against catalogued transitions at the
stellar velocity, and unattributed ones sought again in later blocks. Depth
is a measured recovery fraction: for a carrier narrower than the instrumental
channel, nine injections in ten return at
$\EirpNinetySysMedA$\,W for the median system,
$\EirpNinetySysLoA$--$\EirpNinetySysHiA$\,W across systems. The drift ceiling
is \DriftCeilLo\,nHz, \AccelSheikhPct{} per cent of the \citet{Sheikh2019}
range. Of the \EvNCrossRaw{} crossings, \MkAttr{} fall on a catalogued
transition and \MkUnattr{} do not; \PrFlagXrossWord{} are in one
block failing a quality criterion applied post-inspection. None of the
unattributed crossings recurred, including \PrRankClause, while
\MkAttrRecur{} attributed to circumstellar or foreground carbon monoxide do
recur, as expected. That excludes a carrier holding its stellar-frame
frequency to within $\pm\RqDvMed$\,km\,s$^{-1}$, radiating through both
observations, not a persistent transmitter of any kind; the remainder is
consistent with chance to the control positions' accuracy. Line
confusion costs \MkMaskAPct{} per cent of the coverage and \ScSysNoConf{}
systems have no confirmation epoch; these, not sensitivity, limit the search.
\end{abstract}
