\begin{abstract}
We search \NWinA{} fine-channel ALMA archive windows toward \NSysClassA{}
nearby systems, \CvUnionA\,GHz of union bandwidth, for an unresolved
carrier, plus \NWinB{} coarser windows.
Injections transferred from \NInjWinA{} windows estimate the median system's
best window at $\EirpNinetySysMedA$\,W nine times in ten in one observation,
for a carrier narrower than the instrumental channel drifting
below \DriftCeilLo\,nHz, \AccelSheikhPct{} per cent of the ceiling
\citet{Sheikh2019} recommend. It is a trigger sensitivity, not a confirmation
limit, and excludes atmospheric decorrelation, whose net bias runs either way
by window.
No confirmed carrier is found. Of the \EvNCrossRaw{} threshold crossings --
channels reaching the trigger -- \MkAttr{} are attributed to a catalogued
transition at the star's velocity, \MkUnattr{} are not, \PrFlagXrossWord{} of
them failing a quality criterion.
None of the \RcNExclPoss{} unattributed crossings with a repeat reading
recurred, including \PrRankClause.
In \HjNPowerHi{} of \RcNExcl{} a repeat would show a carrier whose
stellar-frame frequency returns to within $\pm\RqDvLo$--$\RqDvHi$\,km\,s$^{-1}$
(median $\pm\RqDvMed$) radiating through both observations -- not a persistent
transmitter of any kind; for \HjNPowerLo{} they are too shallow to test at the
trigger amplitude.
Their count, \CeObs{} against \CeExp{} expected from the fields' control
positions, is consistent with chance and does not exclude up to \CeAddLim{}
added events. A calibration residual could mimic a carrier in \CxNAdmit{}
windows, measured to act in none.
What limits the search is coverage, not depth: \ZcNoRepSys{} systems have no
confirmation epoch; line emission explains the strongest.
\end{abstract}
