\section{Radio-frequency interference screening}
\label{app:rfi}

Interference is the first alternative explanation for an unattributed
crossing. An interferometer is a poor detector of it -- a signal not in the
far field at the phase centre is suppressed in the correlation by its rapidly
varying geometric phase -- though allocations exist across the searched bands
\citep{ITU2024}, and the documented hazard, spaceborne cloud radar at
\RadarLo--\RadarHi\,GHz \citep{CloudSat2002,EarthCARE2023,ALMAMemo504},
overlaps \RadarNWin{} of the \RadarNTot{} census windows, with
\RadarNCross{} crossings and \RadarNStage{} rank-passing events. The
population tests of Table~\ref{tab:rfitests} find none of the signatures
interference leaves, but \emph{no per-channel interference flag exists, and
none can be recovered}, because the correlator flagging was not retained.
The one concentration a reader will notice, \TlBandAll{} crossings between
\TlBandLo{} and \TlBandHi\,GHz, is the carbon monoxide tuning: \TlBandObs{} of
the \TlBandNSub{} unattributed crossings the census and the reserved hold-out
place in a searched window, against \TlBandNull$\,\pm\,$\TlBandNullSd{}
expected from where the windows were tuned. The image-sideband test is a
bound, not a clearance, and \ImNDsb{} crossings lie where the receivers are
double-sideband (\texttt{documentation/\allowbreak appendix\_E\_rfi.md} of the
data archive, \citealt{SIdeposit}).
