\section{The false-alarm expectation and the control ensemble}
\label{app:falsealarm}

\subsection{The expectation, and what it is conditional on}

\emph{One attribution set serves both sides of the comparison}: the mask of
Appendix~\ref{app:linemask}, so a control maximum inside it is attributed
exactly as a crossing there is. Each of the \CeNWin{} Class~A windows of the
primary census contributes the fraction of its own \NCtrl{} control positions
that reaches the trigger -- no cell count enters it and no form is assumed
for the tail -- and summed over the windows \CeExpTrig{} are expected to
trigger by chance. How many of those would be attributed is measured on the
channels the control maxima land on, which exist only for the \RcNRetCtrl{}
control positions per window that retain a dynamic spectrum
(\S\ref{sec:vistest}): \CeNExcPos{} positions in \CeNExcWin{} windows,
carrying \CeExcCovPct{} per cent of the expectation and indistinguishable from
their window's full ensemble in rank ($P=\CeRetKsP$) and in exceedance rate
(\CeRetZ{} standard deviations). \CeNExcAttr{} of their \CeNExc{} exceedances
fall inside the mask, carried to the star's frame exactly as a stellar cell,
so the attribution takes \CeMaskPct{} per cent of the expectation where a
uniform landing would take \CeUnifPct. The atmospheric screen
(Appendix~\ref{app:rfi}) then takes \CeTlCtrlCut{} of \CeTlCtrlSurv, leaving
\CeExp{} expected unattributed Class~A crossings against \CeObs{} observed.
Every control rate is measured on the data its window's statistic is read
from, including the \CeNRepWinA{} Class~A windows with recombined in-beam
fields; on the first extraction the expectation would be \CeRepDExp{} lower.
The \CeNWinTail{} Class~A windows of the archival tail, with its
\CeNTailCrossWord{} unattributed crossings toward \CeTailStar, and the
\CeNWinComp{} of \OmXStar{} enter neither side.

\emph{A count is compared with the distribution of counts chance delivers.}
Over \CeNBoot{} resamplings -- each window's rate and attributed share redrawn
from its own probes, and the execution blocks resampled because windows share
them -- a count of \CeObs{} or more arises with probability \CeProbNull{},
conditional on the control-position model and not on an independently
established null. Added to each resampled rate, \CeAddLim{} further crossings
still leave \CeObs{} credible at \CeProbAtLim{} and one more does not
(\CeProbAboveLim{} against \CeAlphaPct{} per cent). Resampling blocks rather
than windows widens the scatter by a factor \BgBlockSdRatio, and a pseudo-star
read at one control position in every window of a block gives a count no more
dispersed than one read window by window (variance ratio \BgDisp) and reaches
\CeObs{} with probability \BgPseudoP, so the Poisson step is not optimistic;
these checks, the comparison by configuration and the phase-centre split are
archived with the data \citep{SIdeposit}.

\emph{The one assumption the searched data cannot test is that a star behaves
like a control position in its own field.} The star carries \CeNear{} times a
control position's cells in the band $\CeNearBandLo\le T<\CeNearBandHi$
(\CeNearIntLo--\CeNearIntHi{} at \CeCovPct{} per cent, blocks resampled).
Calibrating the stellar rate against that ratio instead, the sensitivity test
of \S\ref{sec:trigunif}, lowers the bound to \CeAddLimNear; the whole-beam
conclusion is unchanged under it, and the control positions' own rate is
adopted as the more demanding of the two for the count under test. The
\CeObs{} would stand above chance at \CeAlphaPct{} per cent only if stars
triggered at \CeRCrit{} times a control position's rate or less, and every
ratio measured is an excess in the census. The reserved blocks trigger in
\StHoObs{} of \StHoNFine{} windows against \StHoExpFine{} predicted, and the
\HsIndBlocks{} sharing no session with a searched block hold \HsIndObsWord{}
trigger \HsIndObsNoun{} against \HsIndExp{} expected, so they neither confirm
nor contradict the ratio (Appendix~\ref{sec:heldout}).

Near a band edge the pooled exceedance rate is $\times\VnEdgeRatio$ the
interior rate, but the window toward HD~48370 contributes \VnEdgeDomPct{} per
cent of every near-edge exceedance in the survey; without it the ratio is
$\times\VnEdgeNoDomRatio$, and on a held-out half $\times\VnEdgeHalfCleanRatio$
($\pv{\VnEdgeHalfCleanP}$). No edge correction is therefore applied.

\emph{What the rank resolves.} $1/(\NCtrl+1)=\RankFloorVal$ is the control
ensemble's \emph{resolution}, not the probability that noise produced the
window: a survey of \NWindows{} windows would need a rank \BonfRankRatio{}
times finer, and correlated controls inside the beam leave an annulus
resolving no better than \RankEffLo--\RankEffHi. No window can be significant
on the rank alone. The widened recurrence window of \S\ref{sec:etacrv} is
counted in windows, a window covering several crossings being one
measurement: the \WuNRead{} pairings are \WuNWin{} distinct windows, each
one's chance formed from its independent channel--drift cells; with the rate
taken from its own control positions instead the expectation is
\WuExpWinCtrl{} ($P=\WuProbWinCtrl$), and the larger $P$ is quoted. The full treatment is in
\texttt{documentation/\allowbreak appendix\_D\_false\_alarm.md} of the data
archive \citep{SIdeposit}.

\subsection{Execution-block quality}
\label{sec:blockquality}

The comparison assumes each block's control ensemble behaves like noise, and
one block makes that false: all \NCtrl{} of its probes exceed the trigger, the
smallest at \BqFailCtrlMin. Every block is therefore judged on the median of
its windows' control ensembles divided by its class median, which also flags
\DqNWin{} of the \NWindows{} windows individually at \BqFactor{} the survey
median. \emph{The criterion is post hoc}, written after the condition was
seen. It is trustworthy because the failing block sits at \BqFailQ{} and the
next at \BqNextQ, a factor \QsRatio{} apart, and all \QsNStep{} thresholds
between select the same block. \BqNFail{} of \BqNBlocks{} census blocks fails;
its \BqFailNWin{} coarse-channel windows hold no census crossing once ALMA's
final flags are restored (\S\ref{sec:method}).

\subsection{The registration of the predicted cell}
\label{sec:regbudget}

The recurrence test of \S\ref{sec:etacrv} reads one channel at one drift,
registered between epochs to \RgBudgetChMed{} of a channel in every window,
the frame correction moving it by up to \ZtFrameChanMax{} channels. Widening
each reading to the worst cell within \RgTolKms\,km\,s$^{-1}$, pinning it at
every anchor along the discovery track, or raising the bar to \ZtTrialThr{}
changes no disposition. Registration to a single channel is tested only on
the \RgRegNUnres{} recovery readings of \RgRegStar, whose line is confined to
one channel and registers to \RgRegMaxCh{} of a channel; the other recoveries
test the frame correction only to the width of a resolved line.

\subsection{From the widened window's exceedances to the pairs}
\label{sec:pairacct}

Two counts of \S\ref{sec:etacrv} are counts of different objects. The
\WdNExc{} above-trigger readings are window readings, one per pairing of an
unattributed crossing with a window covering its frequency in another block
-- \WdNCov{} such pairings over the \PaNCovWinCross{} crossings a later
catalogued block covers -- so a pair of unattributed crossings is read twice:
\PaNPairTwice{} readings are second readings of \PaNPairRead{} pairs,
\PaNPairBeyond{} outside the interval the pair test admits and
\PaNPairInside{} inside it, the set of Table~\ref{tab:pairs}, the other
\TjNCandLost{} of the \TjNCandPair{} wanting a discovery spectrum or a fitted
drift. The accounting for each pair is
\texttt{candidates/\allowbreak drift\_pair\_accounting.csv} of the data
archive. Of the \TjNPower{} pairs whose epochs are close enough together for
the predicted offset to be tighter than the searched window, every one fails
a drift changing at a constant rate, by \TjSigMin{} times that resolution in
the mildest case. The control row is a line that does not drift: the
\TjCtlLine{} line of \TjCtlStar{} is recovered at one stellar-frame frequency
\TjCtlDtD{} days apart, within \TjCtlNSig{} of the comparison's resolution.

$\beta$~Pictoris is the case the comparison cannot dismiss. Its two
crossings, \TjBpDvKms\,km\,s$^{-1}$ apart in the star's frame in blocks
\TjBpDtH\,h apart, miss a constant acceleration by \TjBpNSig{} times the
resolution, but the mean acceleration they need, \TjBpAccelReq\,m\,s$^{-2}$,
is inside the \WdAGrid\,m\,s$^{-2}$ ceiling and a circular orbit about the
\EcMassBp\,M$_\odot$ star \citep{Crifo1997} reproduces all three numbers the pair fixes, so the pair is not
separable (the orbit fit is in this appendix's full treatment in the data
archive).

\subsection{The drift grid's edges}
\label{sec:edge}

The drift trials run from $-\dot\nu_{\max}$ in half-channel steps to the
first trial at or beyond $+\dot\nu_{\max}$, so the searched interval is
asymmetric by up to one step; \EgNEdge{} of the \EgNDrift{} crossings with a
fitted drift, in \EgDriftPop{}, lie on an outermost trial (Table~\ref{tab:edge}), toward
\EgEdgeStars, and are reported as lower bounds, \EgNEdgeOut{} of them carrying
no amplitude bound, which is why the table reaches beyond
\S\ref{sec:etacrv}'s range.
