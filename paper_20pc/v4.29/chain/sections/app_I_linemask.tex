\section{The line mask}
\label{app:linemask}

A crossing that falls on a known molecular transition has an astrophysical
explanation to hand, and the mask says which crossings those are.

\emph{The list, by one rule.} The mask holds every transition of the
\FrNSpec{} species these setups cover that are detected in discs or molecular
clouds -- the three CO isotopologues, HCN, HCO$^{+}$, CS, CN, SiO, H$_2$CO, SO,
[C\,\textsc{i}] and hydrogen recombination -- inside the \McNIsland{}
searched intervals below \McEuMax\,K in upper-state energy, \FrNTrans{}
transitions (Table~\ref{tab:masklines}), main hyperfine components only and no
strength cut, from one Splatalogue query \citep{Splatalogue2007} of CDMS and
JPL \citep{Pickett1998,CDMS2005} dated \MkListDate.

\emph{The windows were placed for the observers' own lines, and that is a
limitation}: \TnCenPct{} per cent of the \NWinA{} Class~A windows are centred
within $\pm\MaskHalfKms$\,km\,s$^{-1}$ of a listed transition, against
\TnCtrlCenLo--\TnCtrlCenHi{} per cent for the same windows displaced, so
\emph{unattributed} means unattributed against a list these setups shaped.
The list is not the tuning, though: in the \TuNWinSetup{} windows whose setup
the archive records, the observers named the species in \TuNPairTuned{} of
the \TuNPair{} cases of a listed species inside a window, and in
\TuNSOTuned{} of the \TuNSOWin{} windows holding an SO transition. A wider
list, every transition below \XlEuMax\,K of all \XlNSpecies{} species with an
interstellar or circumstellar detection, would cover \XlBandPct{} per cent of
the Class~A union and attribute every unattributed crossing and
\XlShareWidePct{} per cent of the control exceedances alike, so at that
density proximity is not an identification. The closest approach to one, the
\ZmNCross{} unattributed crossings of $\beta$~Pictoris \ZmDvLo{} and
\ZmDvHi\,km\,s$^{-1}$ from methanol $5_K\to4_K$ components, fails three
spectroscopic tests
(\texttt{documentation/\allowbreak appendix\_C\_line\_mask.md} of the data
archive, \citealt{SIdeposit}).

\emph{Attribution is decided in the star's own rest frame.} A crossing's
frequency is carried from the topocentric value to the barycentre using its
own block's pointing and mid-time, and then to the stellar rest frame using
the star's systemic velocity; an offset $\Delta v_\star =
c\,(\nu_\star-\nu_0)/\nu_0$ is positive for a crossing above the transition
in frequency. \emph{Every crossing is also read in the local standard of rest,
where interstellar gas is at rest.} Toward \LsCloudStars{} the CO($1\to0$)
survey of \citet{DameThaddeus2022,Dame2001} shows emission at the crossing's
own LSR velocity (within \LsCloudDvMax\,km\,s$^{-1}$ of its peak,
$\ge$\LsCloudTMin\,K), so \LsNCloud{} of the \MkNCoinc{} coincidences are
interstellar; elsewhere the Planck CO map \citep{PlanckCO2016} stays below
\LsPlanckMax\,K\,km\,s$^{-1}$. HD~48370's coincidence is
\LsHdDvStar\,km\,s$^{-1}$ from the star's own CO($2\to1$) but at
\LsHdVfeat\,km\,s$^{-1}$ in the LSR on a cloud line peaking at \LsHdVco, so
its extended emission is a cloud and not a disc.

\emph{The coincidences.} \MkNCoinc{} of the
\CsNCrossQp{} quality-passing census crossings fall within the mask in their own star's frame, and these
are the \MkAttr{} attributions. The \SoNCrossWord{} on sulphur monoxide
(\SoStars, at \SoDvList\,km\,s$^{-1}$) rest on the coincidence alone: in the
LSR their offsets are \SoDvLsrList\,km\,s$^{-1}$, none within the cloud test,
and no CO($3\to2$) feature above the trigger accompanies any.
The hold-out is treated identically, \HoNAttr{} attributed and \HoNUnattr{}
unattributed (\S\ref{sec:holdoutsearch}).

\emph{The half-width, and what it costs.} Keplerian motion where these
molecules survive, $\lesssim$\EcKepVKms\,km\,s$^{-1}$ beyond \EcKepRAu\,au, sets it at
$\pm\MaskHalfKms$\,km\,s$^{-1}$. \emph{The cost is stated on the union of
searched sky frequencies}: the frequencies within that half-width of a
listed transition's rest frequency, merged where transitions overlap, are \MkMaskAGHz\,GHz of the
\CvUnionA\,GHz Class~A union, \MkMaskAPct{} per cent, and \FrMaskLostGHz\,GHz
of \FrMaskUnionGHz\,GHz over both classes, \FrMaskPct{} per cent; the wider
list's share is measured the same way. A union over windows toward different
stars has no single rest frame, so this describes the coverage and not any one
star; a limit is formed instead over its own window's channels outside the
mask in that star's own frame (\S\ref{sec:summary}).

Of the \MkAttrExcl{} labelled crossings not recovered (\S\ref{sec:etacrv}),
one rests on a velocity instead, its sightline carrying interstellar emission
\LsLupDv\,km\,s$^{-1}$ from the crossing, with \RqAjNgeLo--\RqAjNgeHi{} of
the \NCtrl{} control positions above the star in every repeat block.
