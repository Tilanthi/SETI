\subsection{Frequency frames, linewidth and smearing}
%% (subsection label retired: nothing cites it)

Every frequency axis here is \emph{topocentric}, as the correlator delivers
it; velocities enter only at vetting, where the line mask carries each
crossing to the stellar frame (Appendix~\ref{app:linemask}). EIRP$_{\rm trig}$
is the minimum \emph{total} power of a carrier confined to one native channel,
never a spectral power density, as are the Hz-resolution surveys' limits in
Fig.~\ref{fig:context}(a). Spreading it over $N$ channels lowers the statistic as
$N^{\ScNBroadExp}$, so a feature as wide as the widest line these fields
resolve (\ScNBroadChan{} channels) must carry \ScNBroadRatio$\times$ a
carrier's power.

ALMA's online Hanning smoothing \citep{ALMAHandbook} leaves the peak channel
\HanBest{} of an unresolved carrier's flux at a channel centre and \HanWorst{}
on a boundary. \emph{That loss enters the limits exactly once.} Each injected
carrier is deposited with the correlator's channel response at a sub-channel
phase drawn afresh and recovered through the unmodified trigger, so the
measured completeness contains the loss and factorises into it:
$\EirpNinetyMultA\,P_{\rm trig}$ is $\times\RpRespFac$ for the channel response
and $\times\RpMarginNinety$ for the margin a peak channel needs above the
nominal trigger for nine carriers in ten to return. $P_{\rm trig}$, $S_{\min}$
and the filled symbols of Fig.~\ref{fig:context}(a) are therefore nominal and
comparable with other programmes' thresholds, while $\mathrm{EIRP}_{90}$
carries the correction.

The factor $\RpRespFac=1/\InjDepMean$ is set by the phase-mean peak-channel
share of the $0.25/0.5/0.25$ kernel, which the injections deposited in every
window. For the \SvNWinNOne{} Class~A windows delivered without online
spectral averaging ($N=1$) that share is \InjShortPct{} per cent short of the
exact \InjRespMean; for the other \SvNWinNAvg{} ($N\geq2$), whose broader
response the injections did not use, it is \SvRespShortPctTwo{} per cent short
of \SvRespMeanTwo. The completeness is conservative by that much: for $N=2$ the exact factor is
$\SvRespFacTwo\,P_{\rm trig}$, which a refit of the campaign's own tones
reproduces. The limits are not corrected for it (\S\ref{sec:pninetysel}); each
system's limit with the exact response is in
\texttt{sensitivity/\allowbreak system\_limits.csv} of the data archive.

\emph{Intra-integration smearing, which the injections do not model,} is
divided out of every limit once as $\eta_{\rm smear}$, the mean channel
response over the sweep within one integration. It exceeds one per cent in
\NmRespNApplied{} windows, reaching $\times\SmcFacWorst$; it is measured for
\SmcNMeas{} of the \NWindows{} windows and bounded for the other \SmcNBound{}
at \SmcDtCeil\,s, the longest integration delivered. A re-scoring of the
injected windows shows that this correction errs the safe way
(\texttt{documentation/\allowbreak appendix\_A\_smearing.md} of the data
archive, \citealt{SIdeposit}).

\subsection{The primary-beam response}
\label{app:pbresp}

The sensitivity quoted for a window is $S_{\min}=5\sigma/A$, where $A$ is the
primary-beam response at the star's offset from the phase centre. Archival
fields are pointed at catalogue J2000 positions, so these high-proper-motion
stars can sit off axis: a median \PbOffMed$''$ and at most \BmOffMax$''$ over
the \PbNWin{} windows, with $A$ falling to \BmRespMin{} and below $0.9$ in
\BmNBelowNine{} of them. \emph{The response applied is a Gaussian of the documented full
width} $\RingPbCoef\,\lambda/D$ \citep{ALMAHandbook} at each window's own dish
diameter. It matches the blocked-aperture response of \textsc{casa}
\citep{CASA2022} to \BmDocAiryPct{} per cent in width; the two part company
with offset, which sets the retention rule of \S\ref{sec:sample}, and
\emph{at the furthest census window, $\BmCensusWorstC\,\lambda/D$, they agree
to \BmCensusWorstPct{} per cent, the largest beam-model uncertainty any
sensitivity here carries.} The correction is applied once, to $S_{\min}$; the
statistic, ranks and attribution are ratios within one window, in which $A$
divides out.

The annual parallax is measured rather than modelled: carriers injected at
the full apparent position of the \SensNReextWin{} furthest-out windows
retain \SensPxSmallMeas{} and \SensPxLargeMeas{} of their amplitude where the
model predicts \SensPxSmallPred{} and \SensPxLargePred, so a model-corrected
limit is shallower than it need be. $\epsilon$\,Eridani, with \BmEpsNOff{} of its
\BmEpsNWin{} windows $\BmEpsCLo$--$\BmEpsCHi\,\lambda/D$ off axis and the
largest parallax in the search, has no parallax record, so its limit is
optimistic by at most \BmEpsPxBoundPct{} per cent.

\emph{The propagation assumes straight-line motion, which a component of a
bound pair does not follow.} \OmNComponent{} of the \OmNStar{} searched
positions (the \OmNCensusStar{} census stars and \OmXStar) have a companion
within \OmRadius{} arcsec \citep{wds,orb6}; the displacement, from a visual
orbit, the separation with masses from \citet{pecaut2013}, or the
proper-motion anomaly \citep{kervella2022}, moves no system's limit by more
than \OmSysMovedMaxPct{} per cent. \OmFlagStars{} have disturbed solutions
with no companion and no Hipparcos epoch, so the \OmNWinFlag{} windows of
those \OmNFlag{} stars carry limits optimistic by an unmeasured amount.
