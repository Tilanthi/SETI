\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusions}

We searched archival ALMA observations of \NSysClassA{} star systems within
40\,pc for narrowband, drifting radio carriers. The fine-channel spectral
windows searched cover \CvUnionA\,GHz of sky frequency in \CovNIslands{}
intervals between \SurvFreqLoA{} and \SurvFreqHiA\,GHz, at least
\SbPriorRatio{} times the bandwidth of either millimetre search of
nearby stars before it \citep{Steffes1994,Mauersberger1996}.

Injection of synthetic carriers into \SensNInj{} windows, transferred to the
rest, gives a median of $\EirpNinetySysMedA$\,W of equivalent isotropic
radiated power for a carrier that a system's best window registers nine times
in ten. Of the \NSysALim{} systems with a constrained window, \NSysEfifteen{}
reach $10^{15}$\,W, or \NmDecAdjNEfifteen{} allowing for atmospheric
decorrelation.

The census holds \CsNCrossQp{} threshold crossings in data that pass the
quality criterion, and \CsNAttrQp{} of them are attributed to catalogued
molecular lines, mostly carbon monoxide. The other \CsNUnattrQp{} are
consistent with chance: control positions beside
each star predict \CeExp{} ($P=\CeProbNull$). Where a crossing's frequency was
observed again, \WuNWinExc{} of the \WuNWin{} repeat windows reach the
threshold against \WuExpWin{} expected ($P=\WuProbWin$), \TwNWinExcTwoWord{} of
them toward two objects. HD~14055's share is explained by its exposure;
\BkAlmaName{} is a target for follow-up, not an established excess, since
chance puts the most discrepant of \TwLookNStar{} objects that far out in
\TwLookPct{} per cent of trials.

No transmitter is detected: of the unattributed crossings whose repeat could
be read, none returns at the frequency its discovery predicts. This is a non-detection,
not an exclusion. The limits apply only to a carrier outside the
molecular-line mask, on while ALMA observed and drifting by at most
\DriftCeilLo--\DriftCeilHi\,nHz (\AccelCeilMed--\AccelCeilMax\,m\,s$^{-2}$;
\S\ref{sec:excludes}). Confirmation, more than sensitivity, limits what the archive can show:
\ZcNoRepSys{} of the \NSysClassA{} systems were never re-observed at a covered
frequency, and the line-recurrence check can recover an emitter at rest in
the star's frame but not a carrier that drifts or is re-tuned between epochs. A dedicated
programme would channelise finely, avoid the bright molecular lines and
revisit each target at one tuning, days and years apart (\S\ref{sec:future}).
