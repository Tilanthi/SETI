# Interference: the full treatment

This file holds the full text of paragraphs the article summarises, as the article argues them, with every number taken from the same computation the article prints. Where the article points here, the summary it keeps is in the paper and the complete argument is below.

Interference is the first alternative explanation for an unattributed crossing, so the screening this survey has and the coverage it lacks are both set out here. Millimetre interferometry is an unusually poor environment for terrestrial interference, though not an empty one: ITU Radio Regulations Article 5 (International Telecommunication Union 2024) carries active allocations across the searched bands, while an interferometer is not a total-power detector, so a signal not in the far field at the phase centre acquires a rapidly varying geometric phase across the array and is suppressed in the correlation rather than imaged. The documented hazard is spaceborne cloud radar in the 94.0–94.1 GHz Earth exploration-satellite allocation (Stephens et al. 2002; Wehr et al. 2023; Emerson 2004): 1 of the 1663 census windows overlap it, carrying 0 threshold crossings and 0 rank-passing events, so no result here rests on it. *The five tests of the table of the paper headed “The interference tests, each of which could have failed” carry none of the signatures interference leaves, and their coverage is uneven.* Each is computed for every searched window of the census, as is the 512-position rank screen, while recurrence reaches only the crossings with a covering repeat block; and because the observatory's own correlator flagging was not retained window by window, *no per-channel interference flag of our own exists, and none can be recovered* – which is also why the five populations differ, each test needing a different quantity to have been recorded. The clustering the crossings do show is the attribution seen from the other side: the one concentration a reader will notice, 14 crossings between 344.2 and 346.4 GHz, is the carbon monoxide tuning, 8 of the 30 unattributed crossings the census and the reserved hold-out together place in a searched window (row 2.4 of `catalogues/count_accounting.csv`) against 7.0 ± 0.7 expected from where the spectral windows were tuned, and that interval holds 0 telluric transitions. The mask must precede the occupancy screen, because 35 of the 109 above-trigger windows sit on a masked transition and a carbon monoxide line falls at nearly the same sky frequency toward every nearby star, which is the coincidence that screen is looking for; applied, it reads 1 against 2.1 ± 1.3. No crossing lies inside the atmospheric screen's radius; the same radius is taken off the control side as one pooled surviving fraction, so the residual asymmetry runs against the null of the section of the paper on “The false-alarm expectation and the control ensemble” and not for it. The fifth test bounds the one mechanism that could put a crossing where no line is catalogued: the first local oscillator is not delivered, but each block's own spectral windows pin ν_LO1 to a median 1.13 GHz and so place every crossing's image 8–24 GHz away, never inside its own window, while 0 of the 26 survive on frequency alone with the interval taken 1 GHz wider at each end, so it is a bound and not a clearance, and 0 crossings lie where the receivers are double-sideband and no such bound could be drawn. Where a carbon monoxide isotopologue does fall in the image band, 9 crossings lie at the image position against 10.8 expected, and the parent of the unattributed one among them, toward ALMA J153702653-33192492, does not attribute it: it was observed at its own frequency in the same block with nothing at the trigger, the largest amplitude 1.37 times the crossing's own where a leak at the 10 dB image rejection ALMA specifies would need 10 times as much.

*None of this substitutes for the ON/OFF cadence a dedicated search uses*: allocation tables cannot exclude harmonics, intermodulation products, unconventional emitters or near-field sources, and archival data without a simultaneous off-source strategy cannot do better. These are statements about the population and not a clearance of any one crossing.
