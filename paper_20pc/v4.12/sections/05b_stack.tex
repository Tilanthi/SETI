\subsection{A stellar-frame multi-epoch stacked search}
\label{sec:stack}

Combining the epochs of a star in that star's own rest frame deepens the
limit. \SxStkStarCensus{} of the surveyed stars, lying in \SxStkSysCensus{}
separate systems, hold more than one searched execution block. Over
\SyStkNGroup{} stacked groups, \SyStkNEpochComb{} epoch-windows and
\SyStkHours\,h, the flux limit improves by a median factor \SyStkGainMed{}
over each star's best single epoch (\SyStkGainPNinety{} at the 90th
percentile, \SyStkGainMax{} at most). \SxStkStarOutside{} further stars
stack as well --- \SyStkStarOos{} control stars beyond 40\,pc, and
\SyStkStarOther{} star covered only by archival blocks that enter no census
statistic --- and are kept out of those figures: over all \StkNStar{} stacked
stars the median gain falls to \StkLimitGainMed{}, the extra stacks being
shallow two-epoch ones, and no limit quoted anywhere in this paper rests on
them. Every stacked limit is measured by injection into the stack itself, on
the same completeness convention as the rest of the paper, and never read off
a stacked spectrum as a threshold.

\emph{A fixed stellar-frame frequency is not a fixed observed frequency, so
the epochs are registered before they are added.} The barycentric term alone
swings by a median \StkVcorrSwingMed\,km\,s$^{-1}$ between the epochs of one
star and by as much as \StkVcorrSwingMax --- many channels at the
\StkRefChanKHz\,kHz Class~A width --- so epochs added as observed would dilute
rather than reinforce. Each epoch is transformed to the stellar rest frame,
divided by its own primary-beam response and combined by inverse variance
over the channels of common stellar-frame coverage, keyed on the star's
catalogued identity rather than the directory the search ran in. Spectra
whose archived visibility weights are pathological are excluded first
(\StkBadWeight{} of \StkNProd, which would otherwise take almost all the
weight). The transform is verified on a real line: it collapses HD~285968's
molecular line from a \StkRegTopoChan-channel topocentric spread to
\StkRegStellarChan{} channels, an rms of $\StkRegRmsKms$\,km\,s$^{-1}$, over
\StkRegNEpoch{} epochs spanning \StkRegSpanD\,d.

The criterion was fixed before any stellar position was examined: a stacked
$Z\ge\StkZmin$ above every stacked control maximum, no epoch supplying more
than \StkDomMax{} of the weighted sum, and at least \StkJkMin{} of the signal
surviving removal of the strongest epoch. Controls are stacked identically
and every clause evaluated at them, which measures the whole test's
false-alarm rate at \StkCtrlFap{} per cent against a \StkFapCeil{} per cent
ceiling fixed in advance.

\emph{The gain is $\sqrt{N_{\rm eff}}$ and not $\sqrt{N}$}, where
$N_{\rm eff}=\sigma_{\min}^{2}\sum_e\sigma_e^{-2}$ is the equivalent number of
equal-depth epochs and the median $N_{\rm eff}/N$ is \StkNeffFrac. Measured
against $\sqrt{N_{\rm eff}}$ the combination is ideal to \StkGainVsNeff{} in
the median, with a per-stack spread of $\pm$\StkMadZScatterPct{} per cent that
is the sampling error of a noise scale measured over \StkNChanMed{} channels
and nothing to do with stacking. Against $\sqrt{N}$ it is \StkGainVsN, the
shortfall being unequal epoch depth, so an epoch count alone misleads:
\StkWorstStar{} stacks \StkWorstN{} epochs for a gain of \StkWorstGain,
because its $N_{\rm eff}$ is \StkWorstNeff.

\emph{The deepest stacked limit is Proxima Centauri's, and it is a Class~B
limit.} Proxima holds \StkProxN{} blocks --- \SxProxEbCensus{} primary-census blocks
together with \SxProxEbExt{} archival blocks observed after the census was
frozen --- over
\StkProxHours\,h spanning \StkProxSpanYr\,yr, all of them coarse
\ChanBModeMHz\,MHz windows, so the stack constrains an unresolved spectral
excess and discriminates no drift. Its $N_{\rm eff}$ is only \StkProxNeff,
so what combining those epochs buys is
$\sqrt{N_{\rm eff}}=\SxProxSqrtNeff$. Carriers injected into every epoch and
recovered through the same path give a 90 per cent recovery point at
\StkCalMult$\times$ the stack's nominal threshold --- a property of the
stacking chain rather than of this star, since a second group returns
\StkCalMultTwo$\times$ --- and on that one convention the best single epoch
reaches $\StkProxEirpSingle$\,W and the stack
$\mathrm{EIRP}_{90}=\StkProxEirpCal$\,W, a ratio of \StkProxGain. The excess
over $\sqrt{N_{\rm eff}}$ is this spectrum's own noise, \SyProxNoiseMeas{} of
the inverse-variance prediction across its \SxProxNChan{} common channels,
well inside the spread quoted above and not a gain beyond the root-$N$ law.
The limit holds for an emitter steady in the stellar frame to
\StkProxTolKms\,km\,s$^{-1}$ --- one \ChanBModeMHz\,MHz channel --- over
epochs spanning \StkProxSpanYr\,yr, with no inter-epoch drift above
\StkProxDriftHzS\,Hz\,s$^{-1}$. At \StkProxDistPc\,pc that depth is mostly
geometry; the gain is what stacking bought.

\emph{\StkNSurvive{} groups pass the full criterion and both are known
lines.} They are $\beta$~Pictoris' CO $J=1\!-\!0$ at
\StkBpCoOneOff\,km\,s$^{-1}$ over \StkBpCoOneN{} epochs ($Z=\StkBpCoOneZ$)
and its $J=2\!-\!1$ at \StkBpCoTwoOff\,km\,s$^{-1}$ ($Z=\StkBpCoTwoZ$),
agreeing to \StkBpCrossKms\,km\,s$^{-1}$ or \StkBpCrossChan{} of a CO(1--0)
channel: the astrophysical positive control passing in the stack.
Attributing every survivor against the survey's own
$\pm\StkAttrKms$\,km\,s$^{-1}$ mask leaves \StkNUnattributedComputed{}
unattributed. The stack is also an independent test of the threshold
crossings of \S\ref{sec:etacrv}: the \StkNPersistStar{} stars carrying them
stack to $Z=\StkPersistZLo$--\StkPersistZHi{} in \StkNPersistBelow{} cases,
\StkNPersistAboveCtrl{} exceed their own control ensembles and
\StkNPersistSurvive{} passes the criterion. The exception,
\StkPersistLineStar, reaches $Z=\StkPersistLineZ$ on the foreground line
used above as the registration standard and is rejected because its own
controls reach \StkPersistLineCtrl: the emission is extended, as a cloud is
and a transmitter is not.

\emph{The restriction travels with every number above, and its width is the
channel.} At CO(2--1) one \StkRefChanKHz\,kHz channel is
\StkTolFineKms\,km\,s$^{-1}$, so the $\pm\StkReflexAmpKms$\,km\,s$^{-1}$
reflex swing of a transmitter on a short-period giant planet spans
\StkReflexChan{} of them and de-registers itself completely, against only
\StkReflexChanCoarse{} channels of a \ChanBModeMHz\,MHz window: the coarse
stacks buy reflex tolerance with bandwidth. Drift divides the same way: in
\StkRefChanKHz\,kHz channels a stack is confined to
\StkDriftTolHzS\,Hz\,s$^{-1}$ over the median inter-epoch span of
\StkSpanMedD\,d. Each stacked limit therefore
constrains emitters stable in the stellar frame to within its own channel and
nothing else; the single-epoch limits of \S\ref{sec:excludes} apply
generally.
