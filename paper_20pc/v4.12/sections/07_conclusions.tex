\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusions}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em,itemsep=4pt,topsep=4pt,parsep=0pt]

\item Three statements fix how new this range is. No published radio
technosignature search above about 30\,GHz has reached a star within 40\,pc. The one published search in that range targeted stars at
\MasonNearKpc\,kpc and beyond. And of the \UnionGHz\,GHz of unique sky
frequency searched here in either class, \UnionBandThree\,GHz lies inside the
frequency axis of the \citet{Wright2018} search haystack, which defines a
parameter space rather than coverage by any telescope.

\item The primary experiment is \NWinA{} fine-channel windows toward
\NSysClassA{} stellar systems, covering \CvUnionA\,GHz of union bandwidth in
disconnected intervals, searched for an unresolved carrier whose frequency
drifts. A supplementary coarse-channel search for an unresolved spectral
excess adds \NWinB{} windows toward \PrSysClassB{} systems and
\CvUnionB\,GHz of union bandwidth, with no drift discrimination. Both come
from one uniform reduction of \NEB{} public execution blocks,
\TotalDataTB\,TB of raw data.

\item Sensitivity is a measured completeness, not a threshold. Carriers
injected into the real calibrated visibilities are recovered through the
unchanged selection --- the trigger, and localisation at the star --- and
$\mathrm{EIRP}_{90}$ is the power at which nine in ten come back. The median
system's best window reaches $\EirpNinetySysMedA$\,W, with a spread across
systems of $\EirpNinetySysLoA$--$\EirpNinetySysHiA$\,W. The rank and the
visibility fit decide nothing, so neither is charged to that limit.

\item All \NCross{} threshold crossings are reported with their dispositions.
Of these, \NAttributed{} coincide with a catalogued molecular transition,
almost all of them carbon monoxide --- including the $\beta$~Pictoris disc,
which the search recovered without being told where to look. Of the remaining
\NUnattributed{}, \CnNTested{} were tested for recurrence in the star's own
frame, at every later observation that covered the event frequency: none is
present again, the largest statistic at any predicted cell being \RcTMax{}
against $T_{\rm trig}=\StageNullTrig$. For \CnNNoRecur{} of them that yields a
quoted exclusion, the weakest \RcExclMin$\sigma$; for the other
\CnNFlaggedWord{} no exclusion is quoted, because their discovery block fails
the data-quality criterion (Appendix~\ref{sec:blockquality}) and a
significance inherited from a defective block would mean nothing. The
\CnNNoSpecWord{} crossing not tested had not retained its discovery spectrum,
so there was no
amplitude to carry into the epochs that do cover it. Just
\PrRankWord{} of the \NUnattributed{} are brighter at their star than at all
\NCtrl{} control positions of their own field, which on its own decides
nothing, and neither recurs.

\item What this survey could search and what it could confirm are different
extents. The carrier search covered
\RcSearchSys{} systems, of which only \RcConfSysDay{} --- carrying
\CvConfPctDay{} per cent of the searched union bandwidth --- were observed
again at the same frequency more than a day apart, and \RcConfSysYr{} beyond
a year. Across both classes, \RcNoRepSysAll{} of the \RcSearchSysAll{}
systems have no second block covering a frequency the first one covered, so a
crossing toward one of them could not be retested from the archive at any
strength: it would remain an untested crossing rather than a limit until a
matched re-observation existed, and the null reported here does not cover
that case.

\item Combining each surveyed star's epochs in its own rest frame deepens
the flux limit by a median factor \SyStkGainMed. For Proxima~Centauri,
where $\sqrt{N_{\rm eff}}=\SxProxSqrtNeff$ is what combining the epochs
buys, the stacked limit measured by injection into the stack reaches
$\StkProxEirpCal$\,W in \ChanBModeMHz\,MHz channels --- a Class~B limit on
an unresolved excess, carrying no drift discrimination --- for an emitter
steady in the stellar frame to \StkProxTolKms\,km\,s$^{-1}$ across
\StkProxSpanYr\,yr.

\item These results constrain persistent, unresolved carriers only within
the domain actually searched and only toward this sample
(\S\ref{sec:excludes}). The more transferable result is what such a search
runs into. For classifying a crossing in the present archival data set,
molecular-line confusion and sparse repeat coverage are more immediate
limitations than thermal sensitivity, and a channelisation chosen by other
observers for other purposes then decides what kind of search is possible at
all --- while the power a transmitter must radiate to be found here remains
enormous.

\end{enumerate}
