paper v3.88 -- the prospective epoch extension

123 public in-scope execution blocks that the frozen survey never searched
were found and searched. They existed because make_worklist.py had been
built against the v3.43-era archive map (102 member OUS, 448 progenitors);
the v3.81 map (152 OUS, 656) exposed them. They were searched with the
frozen pipeline AFTER the detection statistic and the molecular-line mask
were fixed.

THEY ARE DELIBERATELY NOT MERGED INTO THE SCIENCE SAMPLE. Every statistic
in this paper rests on a denominator and a candidate list fixed before the
data were examined, and merging would destroy that property. The frozen
sample is unchanged; the new data is reported as a prospective extension
(new sec:extension) with its own null, its own generated table, and its
own released file, and the Data Availability statement says why.

The survey's own withholding rule was applied rather than bypassed: all 32
eps Eri windows in the campaign are Band 6, where the star sits near 0.7
primary-beam FWHM and the Gaussian beam correction does not hold, so all
are withheld exactly as in the frozen sample. That leaves 115 blocks, 463
windows (67 Class A), 16 stars, 16 systems, 57.6 GHz.

Three results:
1. The departure from exchangeability REPRODUCES on data the statistic had
   never seen: median add-one rank 0.409 over 463 windows where 0.5 is
   expected, D = 0.116. A third independent measurement after the hold-out
   (0.429) and the radius analysis (0.437), and the strongest support the
   paper has for using the spatial rank as a screen and never as a
   significance.
2. The positive control recovers: 3 windows reach stage 1 and all three
   are the beta Pictoris disc in CO(1-0), T* = 23.17 / 19.82 / 19.39
   against ring maxima below 6.21, within 8.8-9.0 MHz of the transition,
   in three DIFFERENT previously unsearched execution blocks.
3. No new unattributed event: 13 threshold crossings (2.81 per cent), 4
   rank-first against 1.3 expected, 0 unattributed against 0.21 expected
   on the 78 windows whose control maximum reaches the trigger. The
   paper's unattributed count remains 4.

The epoch gain is small and is reported as such: systems with more than
one searched epoch 55 -> 56 of 82; single-epoch systems 27 -> 26. That
matches the prediction S6.4 item (2) made before the campaign ran, and
item (2) now records that it has been acted on in part.
