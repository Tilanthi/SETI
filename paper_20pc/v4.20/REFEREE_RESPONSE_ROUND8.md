## Referee 1

**1 — Class A/B inconsistency (1 MHz vs 5 MHz).** Fixed at the root: the
classes are defined once in §4, and every count, table entry and figure label
is generated from the same pipeline variable (`round8_calc.py` →
`\ChanA*`/`\ChanB*` macros: Class A 15.259 kHz–1.953 MHz, Class B 15.625–31.25
MHz). We chose to keep channelisation as the classifier (it is an observable
of the archive, not an inferred property) and to report the physical drift
parameter separately per class (`\EtaStraddle`, `\EtaAmin…\EtaBgeOneMax`):
six Class A windows have η_drift < 1 and twelve Class B windows η_drift ≥ 1,
now stated at the definition. Text no longer says all Class A windows
"resolve carrier drift" (specific item 3); the per-class resolution
distribution is given (item 4). (The referee-preferred η-based rename of the
classes themselves arrived with round 9's R3-5: "drift-resolved
spectral-carrier search" / "unresolved spectral-excess search".)

**2 — EIRP "limit" language.** Adopted the referee's terminology throughout:
"nominal 5σ trigger EIRP" / "nominal detection threshold"; "bound any
transmitter above them" and its kin are gone; the Conclusions no longer call
the thresholds "limits". The boxed "How to read every limit in this paper"
rule (§4) states the trigger-vs-completeness distinction once, and the
abstract carries it in the referee's prescribed form.

**3 — Multi-configuration injection campaign (4–8 configs). Declined for this
revision**, with the reason: it requires visibility-level end-to-end runs
that this revision environment cannot execute (no cluster access; the
injection machinery lives with the survey processing system). The
stratification plan (band, channel width, t_on, antenna count, PB offset,
randomised drift/channel placement) is retained verbatim as a **requirement
for the full-census paper** (§6.4), and the single-configuration status is
stated at every point of use (abstract, §4.5, figures, Appendix Q bracket),
including in the strongest form the second referee prescribed (A2-ii below).

**4 — Zero-drift fine-window injection. Declined, same reason** as item 3.
The unmeasured cell is named at the point of use, not buried: §4.5 states
that no end-to-end zero-drift injection has been run on a fine window, and
the dwell/frequency-stationary recovery that *is* measured (coarse windows,
real-visibility dwell experiment) is kept distinct from it. The experiment
is pre-registered with the campaign of item 3.

**5 — False-alarm section.** Rewritten around the frozen symmetric statistic
only. The main-text discussion now separates exactly the five stages the
referee listed (rank-first probability; ≥5σ amplitude; line mask;
family-level Monte-Carlo; the 512-control resolution floor), and every number
derived only from the retired asymmetric statistic was moved to the
development-history appendix and labelled historical. The Poisson expectation
431/513 = 0.84 is the single operative pre-mask number.

**6 — CP−72 2713 further checks.** What the frozen products carry was
consolidated and is now argued in one place: the full eight-species mask
audit, the velocity offset from every catalogued transition, the atmospheric
and spw-edge context, and the bootstrapped ring-maxima context. The
visibility-domain tests (channel imaging, phase/localisation fit, per-antenna
and per-polarisation splits, calibrator inspection) and the Gaia/2MASS/WISE
cross-match **are declined for this revision** — they need re-extraction
beneath the frozen products, which this environment cannot run — and the
deferral is justified concretely (round 9's R4-A5 pressed this further; the
v3.39 justification is quoted in the round-9 letter). Round 9 additionally
gave the feature its own subsection and property table (R3-3 there).

**7 — Post-hoc mask, two stages.** Implemented as prescribed: results are
presented first before astrophysical masking (20 ≥5σ on-source windows; four
star-over-control flags), then as astrophysical interpretation (three of four
with independent CO evidence — matched β Pic transitions/velocities/epochs,
independently published HD 48370 foreground CO), with the frozen ±50 km/s
rule explicitly prospective for the confirmatory sample. The β Pic and
HD 48370 identifications are argued from the astrophysical evidence, not
from mask membership.

**8 — Occurrence demoted.** The conditional f95 ≈ 6% number is out of the
headline-quantities table. Eqs 4–5 remain in the main text as framework
only. (Round 9's R3-4 completed this: all numerical demonstrations moved to
Appendix Q, labelled *illustrative calculation only*, with how-to-cite
guidance.)

**9 — EB count reconciliation.** 102 vs 103 reconciled by generated macros
(`\EbsRetained`, `\EbsRankAll`, `\EbsExtraUid`): 103 EBs carry rank
information, 102 are retained after the one excluded block is accounted; the
distinction is now explained at the point of use. Every count in the paper
(census, stars, systems, datasets, windows, extracted rows, EBs) is
generated from the one machine-readable accounting of the frozen export.

**11 — Priority claims.** Rephrased conservatively: "we are unaware of
previous published ALMA technosignature searches of…" (Barnard's Star,
Wolf 359) and "we are unaware of a previous technosignature survey applying
closure phase in this manner". No priority is claimed on the merits.

**12 — Length and repeated caveats.** The "Scope of the constraints" box now
closes §4 and is referred back to by tag thereafter; the abstract was rebuilt
around three results (sample/exposure; zero candidates; nominal trigger
sensitivity with the completeness caveat inline). The near-verbatim caveat
repetition the second referee counted (~a dozen) was consolidated further in
v3.39 (round 9, C2 there).

**A1 — blindness statement for the statistic switch.** Now one consolidated,
unambiguous claim, stated in the abstract ("the mid-analysis statistic
revision was decided on its algebraic merits before any flagged window was
re-examined"), in §5.4 at the event, and in the audit appendix; the statistic
is frozen before the remaining 101 datasets are searched, so the concern

full dated chronology into the main text with the per-flag audit table; see
the round-9 letter.)

**A2 — completeness-transfer prominence. Option (ii) chosen** (option (i) is
the injection campaign of Referee 1 item 3, declined above for this
environment): the abstract itself now states that the recovery curve is
measured on a single fine-channel configuration and transferred by
assumption with unmeasured error, and carries the P50 = 1.10 P_trig /
P90 > 2 P_trig corrections at the point where the thresholds are quoted.
(Round 9's abstract restructure later trimmed P50/P90 from the abstract per
that referee's Prescription, keeping the single-configuration caveat — which
round 9 strengthened to "transfer error unbounded".)

**A3 — ALMA-observed subsample, not the 40 pc population.** One plain
sentence now stands early in the Introduction ("the non-detection
characterises the ALMA-observed subsample, not the 40 pc stellar population;
M dwarfs, which dominate the local volume by number, are under-sampled
×0.6–0.7"), with the ×3.1–3.4 F/G over-representation in the abstract.

**A4 — Class B visual distinction.** All threshold figures (1, 9, 10) now
separate the classes by marker *and* fill *and* legend text naming the
evidential asymmetry ("Class B: unresolved excess, completeness unmeasured"),
and the figure legends were regenerated from the class variable (no
hand-typed counts). The Fig 1 two-panel redesign (round 9) removes the
remaining temptation to read one axis as a sensitivity ranking.

**A5 — partial-release model and citation practice.** §1 justifies the
51%-release model for a general readership (methodology is the citable
content; each release is a frozen snapshot superseded row by row, contrasted
with interim transient-survey releases), and one sentence at the first
headline table states what a citer should do: cite the frozen
commit-tagged snapshot and check for a newer release before quoting a value.

**A6 — EB-correlation term in f95.** Quantified, not asserted: a sensitivity
run with the effective N reduced by the measured intraclass correlation
(`\EffKbar`, `\EffDeff`, `\EffFIccPct`) is reported alongside the 0.5–2×
completeness-transfer bracket, and the term is an order below it.

**B1–B14.** B1 the page-4 collision was a production artefact of the
overfull vbox; cleared (and the 38-page/0-overfull gate now holds — v3.38's
single 4.07 pt page-1 vbox was the last instance, removed in v3.39). B2 the
