## Referee 1

**R1-1 (≈6 % too prominent).** Done. The abstract no longer quotes any
completeness-corrected transmitter fraction. Its third paragraph now reads:
*"The conditional transmitter-fraction machinery is demonstrated, not quoted:
limits follow once completeness is calibrated on the remaining
configurations."* No juxtaposition of "88 processed stars" with completeness
claims remains. "Bounds"/"gives" language for the fraction is gone from the
abstract entirely.

**R1-2 (broader injections or downgrade).** We took the referee's second
branch. The ≈6 % figure survives only in (i) §6.2, where it is labelled a
demonstration and accompanied by a quantitative bracket — the recovery curve
scaled 0.5×/2× (capped at 1) moves f95 to
\OccBracketLo–\OccBracketHi per cent, and the √t_on calibration anchor
shift (factor 1.2) moves it \OccAnchorShift points — and (ii) the summary
Table 5 row "Conditional f95 … (demo.; bracket)". The abstract's
injections sentence now states the transfer explicitly: *"Real-visibility
injections on one fine-channel configuration, transferred by assumption,
give \RecThresh per cent threshold recovery for the drifting class; the
transfer error is unmeasured."*

**R1-3 (coarse amplitude vs drift consistency).** The referee's exact
formulation is adopted in the abstract and §4/§6: coarse windows "retain
sensitivity to persistent unresolved excess power", with no
survey-threshold completeness curve and no drift discrimination. Table 5's
fine 42 %-at-threshold entry and the coarse 83–100 % entry are no longer
presented as parallel: the coarse entry is labelled as the ≥4σ-per-integration
dwell experiment, not a threshold-completeness measurement.

**R1-4 (primary-beam exchangeability).** Softened as suggested: "empirically
estimated local false-alarm distribution" throughout; the analytic
primary-beam argument (one-sided, conservative) remains in §4. The beam-model
computation needs visibility re-extraction, which this release cannot do; the
known residual non-exchangeability is stated as such.

**R1-5 (1/513 floor; terminology).** The floor now appears immediately after
Eq. 1 together with the survey-wide Bonferroni threshold
(1.2×10⁻⁴, two orders below the floor). "Spatially significant hits" is
replaced by "control-ring exceedances satisfying ≥5σ" in Table 5 and
everywhere else.

**R1-6 / R2-8 (CP−72 2713).** Promotion/retirement criteria are now pinned
with numbers in §5.5: promotion requires p < 1.2×10⁻⁴ for this window, which
needs of order 10⁴ control positions (the present ring resolves 1/513);
retirement occurs on a clean second epoch or identification with catalogued
emission. The enlarged local null itself is deferred with stated reasons:
visibility re-extraction is not possible without a fresh archive pull, and the
named 345.1152 GHz re-observation is the direct arbiter.

**R1-7 (developmental vs confirmatory mask).** The distinction appears in the
Table 6 caption and in the abstract ("The detection statistic and line mask
were developed on this release; remaining datasets form the pre-registered
confirmatory sample").

**R1-8 (mask sensitivity).** ±20/30/50/100 km s⁻¹ half-widths, recomputed from
the frozen export by reclassifying all crossings and recomputing masked
bandwidth: the numbers appear in §5.1 (generated macros). Surviving
exceedances and masked-bandwidth fractions are quoted for each width; the
±50 km s⁻¹ choice is shown not to be outcome-driving.

**R1-9 (census terminology).** "ALMA-archive-conditioned census" in the
abstract and introduction; the glossary entry reinforces it.

**R1-10 (exposure-weighted coverage).** C(ν)=Σᵢtᵢ(ν) metrics added in §5.1:
total exposure \ExpoTotalSHG star-hour-GHz, covered-bin median
\ExpoMedSH star-hours, peak \ExpoPeakSH star-hours, and the concentration of
exposure in the top frequency bins — making the fragmentary, tuning-centred
weighting quantitative.

**R1-11 (Fig. 1).** The native-channel-width second axis is present
(verified); the y-axis now also carries the "(nominal, not
completeness-corrected)" sub-label requested by Referee 2.

**R1-12 (sub-channel placement).** EIRP thresholds are quoted to ≤2
significant figures, with an explicit statement that individual thresholds
carry a sub-channel-position systematic of order tens of per cent.

**R1-13 (duty-cycle vocabulary).** Renamed p_epoch (epoch activity
probability) and f_dwell (intra-observation occupancy); "duty cycle" is
reserved for the transmitter. The Bernoulli-independence caveat for
long-ON/OFF transmitters is attached to the f95 ladder.

**R1-14 (length).** The main text was tightened further this round
(~3.5 kcharacters removed: repeated caveat restatements, §5.5 recap
duplications, §7 opening, appendix exclusions). Withdrawn-statistic history
remains quarantined in appendices, as the referee suggested.

**Specific points.** (a) Abstract/88-star juxtaposition removed (above).
(b) Table 5 de-parallelised (above). (c) "control-ring exceedances satisfying
≥5σ" (above). (d) §6.1 τ Ceti now quantitative: a drifting transmitter above

(e) **Fig. 13**: the caption/text inconsistency is fixed. The figure is the
amplitude-level ensemble CDF (all 431 on-star vs 220,672 control amplitudes);

bracket, not tenths. (i) §6.3: the visibility-domain likelihood is elevated

**Seven acceptance conditions.** (1) ≈6 % presentation — corrected (removed
from abstract, demonstration framing). (2) transferred completeness —

done. (5) coarse amplitude/drift consistency — uniform formulation adopted.
(6) Fig. 13 — corrected. (7) streamlining — further trims applied, 38 pages
