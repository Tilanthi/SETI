## 1. ★★★ The primary-beam audit ships as a RESULT, not a repair (D29)

The alarm that opened this thread — a possible survey-wide flux-scale error — **was wrong**,
and what replaces it is a positive statement about the release. New App. A.1,
`\label{app:pbresp}`, generated by `pbaudit_v408.py`.

- The offset is computed for **every** measurement set from its own `FIELD::PHASE_DIR` and
  the propagated stellar position; no window carries a zero-by-assumption offset. Over the
  **1,651** released windows the offset has median **0.089″**, p99 **7.43″**, max **8.13″**;
  A runs from **0.999984** in the median to **0.5522**, below 0.99 on **91** windows and
  below 0.9 on **34**. **None below 0.5**, and every low-response window is a physically
  explicable J2000 pointing on a high-proper-motion star.
- ★★ **Correcting the threshold alone is the complete correction.** `smin = 5·rms/A` closes
  on **1,651 of 1,651** rows to **8.7e−5** relative, asserted in the build — ★ of which
  **1,641 are a test and 10 are arithmetic**, a distinction the first pass of this version
  missed and §1d repairs; and T⋆, the
  control ranks and the attribution are all ratios formed *inside one window*, in which A
  divides out identically. No crossing, no rank, no disposition moves.
- ★★★ **`\NCatCols` 60 → 62** (`pb_offset_arcsec`, `pb_atten`), applied through
  `v342_calc_pbcols_v408.patch`. **Verified: of the 60 pre-existing columns, 0 cells of
  1,651 rows change.** The count is pinned with `==` and driven **both ways** in

  `theta_pb_arcsec` is the pipeline's `1.22 λ/D` at **D = 12 m for every window, including

## 1a. ★★★ R2-3 REVERSES, and it is this version's most important text change (D32)

Referee 2 asked us to *"derive the exclusion level for the two unattributed events from the
measured Re/$\sigma$--$T_\star$ relation"*. **Measured, the relation does not exclude them.**
The paper's "excluded at 3.3–6.1$\sigma$" was not merely unsupported — it was **backwards**.
New prose in **§\ref{sec:technosearch} (main text, two sentences)** and in
**§\ref{sec:vistest} (two paragraphs)**; macros from `p90r7_v406.py`, which now reads
`r8inputs/r23_v408.json`, the released ledger `ledger_v403.json` and the campaign's own
aggregate.

Three things, computed and not transcribed:

1. ★★ **The two estimators DO track, for a real source at the star.** The median
   Re/$\sigma$ per unit $T_\star$ stays between **1.06 and 1.17 in all 7** recovered-$T_\star$
   bins, over a factor of eight in injected amplitude. So the failure to track at
   $T_\star\approx5$ across the crossings is **evidence about those crossings**, not an
   estimator disagreement. Asserted over *every* bin, and driven in **both** directions —
   case 35 tightens the tolerance, case 36 feeds it a quantity that does not track and
   requires it to fire. *(Divergence recorded: `P90_R7_RESULT.md` quotes 1.04–1.10 from the
   by-amplitude table; 1.06–1.17 is the by-recovered-$T_\star$ one, which is the conditioning
   the events are compared under.)*
2. ★★ **The test has the power the paper asserted without measuring.** In the events' own
   bin, $[5,6)$, injected sources give Re/$\sigma$ = **6.58 ± 1.97 over 283 fits**
   (16th–84th 5.11–8.49), **0.986** reaching Re/$\sigma\ge4$ and **0.977** passing all three
   clauses. *(Divergence recorded: the campaign note quotes 6.59 ± 2.04 over 260 fits; the
   deposited aggregate is 283 fits and is what the build uses.)*
3. ★★★ **Applied to the events, it puts them inside that distribution.** **30 of the 33**
   unattributed fitted crossings in the bin localise; the **6** deepest, one per star, return
   **+4.86 to +5.26**, spanning **percentiles 9–23**, with **1** of the 6 above the injected
   16th percentile. The rank-flagged one, **61 Vir**, returns **+5.26** at **percentile 23**.
   Low in the distribution, and **inside** it.

★★★ **And the superseded-epoch values an exclusion rested on are not comparable, which the
build now proves rather than asserts.** The same injected real sources, re-fitted at that
epoch, return a median Re/$\sigma$ of **1.52** and a 16th percentile of **−0.49** — and
61 Vir's own superseded value, read out of the released ledger's `r6` record, is **−0.93**,
*the referee's own number*. A negative value there is what a genuine compact source looks
like under the defect. Nothing about that is typed: the generator selects the rank-flagged
crossing on the screen flag, not by name, and reads both epochs' fits from the ledger.

★★ **The structural point is stated outright: the trigger and the fit are not independent
measurements.** A noise maximum *at* the star has flux at the star, so it too returns
Re/$\sigma\approx T_\star\approx5$; the real part therefore carries almost nothing beyond
the trigger that selected it, and the fit's power is entirely in the two clauses that test
*displacement* — which reject almost nothing here ($|{\rm Im}/\sigma|<3$ for **0.992** of
injected tones at that $T_\star$). Nor is a 5$\sigma$ crossing in a Class A window
surprising: in the campaign's own **uninjected** passes **11 of 12** Class A windows already
contain a **control** position reaching $T=5$, median control maximum **5.99**, against
**1 of 28** for Class B, while the star itself reaches 5 in **3 of 12** — the real CO lines.
Both halves of that contrast are asserted (cases 43, 44).

family again), and the count is pinned with `==` (case 40). The same file carries the
$|{\rm Im}/\sigma|$ fraction and the clean-pass control census, for the same reason.

**Falsifiers, both directions.** Case 41 compares the observed events against the
superseded-epoch *84th* percentile instead of the median and requires the "not comparable"
sentence to become unwritable; case 42 requires *every* event rather than at least one to
reach the injected 16th percentile and requires "consistent, not excluded" to become
unwritable. A claim this load-bearing should not rest on a one-sided check.
