\section{The spatial rank, and what it can support}
\label{app:radius}

The spatial rank asks whether an excess sits at the star or anywhere else in
the field. It is a diagnostic on which no disposition rests
(\S\ref{sec:summary}), and this appendix is the measurement that settles why.

The star lies inside the control annulus at the phase centre, and the
displacement is real: the star carries more above-threshold cells than a
control position at every level below the trigger
(Appendix~\ref{app:falsealarm}). \emph{It is not a primary-beam effect}:
spectra are extracted in apparent flux density from phase-rotated visibilities
(Appendix~\ref{app:conventions}), and a noise rise toward the field edge would
put the stellar rank \emph{above} one half, the opposite of what is measured;
the ensemble's own weak rise with radius is removed by standardising each
probe within its radius bin. The full treatment is in
\texttt{documentation/\allowbreak appendix\_B\_spatial\_normalisation.md} of the data
archive \citep{SIdeposit}.

\emph{A calibration error that varied with frequency would explain the
displacement, and it is the only mechanism on offer.} A bandpass residual, or
a coarse-mode system temperature interpolated near a telluric line
\citep{ALMATsys2018}, survives the per-integration median along frequency,
multiplies the continuum, so acts at the star and not on empty sky, and stands
still in topocentric frequency, stacking at near-zero drift and putting the
stellar rank \emph{below} one half, the sign observed. \emph{Amplitude excludes
it.} A fractional error $\epsilon$ needs $\epsilon=5\sigma/S_{\rm c}$ to reach
the trigger, with the continuum $S_{\rm c}$ measured for \CyNWinMeas{} of the
\CyNWinTot{} windows and checked against independent continuum images. No
window requires as little as the \CySpecTsysLo--\CySpecTsysHi{} per cent a
coarse system temperature leaves at an ozone line -- the least anywhere is
\CyEpsMinPct{} per cent, the median \CyEpsMedPct, and \CyCrossEpsLoPct{} for
the least demanding of the \CyNCrossMeas{} crossings with a continuum -- the
bandpass branch needs $\CyMarginBp\times$ the \CdSpecBpWorst{} per cent quoted
for the worst bands \citep{ALMAKBBandpass,ALMATechNote15}, and \ScNInRad{}
crossing lies within \ScRadMHz\,MHz of an atmospheric transition
(Appendix~\ref{app:rfi}). The stellar statistic over the median control
statistic has median \NsrMed{} (\NsrLo--\NsrHi) over \NsrNWin{} census windows
without an adopted crossing, so the two noise scales agree.

\subsection{The reserved hold-out, and what it calibrates}
\label{sec:heldout}

The statistic and the mask are set on the searched sample and cannot
calibrate themselves, so a further sample is held out by an identifier rule,
$\mathrm{sha256}(\textsc{uid})_{[:8]} \bmod 5 = 0$, with two guards using
identifiers alone: a block on a named list stays in the survey, and no system
loses every block. Its independence of the statistic and the mask is our
assertion, not something a reader can check. \NEbHeldOut{} of
\NEbProcessedAll{} blocks are reserved, \HoWin{} windows toward \HoStars{}
stars, with no star or system lost. Because the rule hashes the block and not
the session, \HsSharePct{} per cent of the reserved set shares calibrators,
weather and setup with a searched block -- a session-level hash would have
been stronger -- so every quantity from it is also given over the
\HsIndBlocks{} blocks that share no session; on those the control level has a
radial slope of $\HoRadSlope\pm\HoRadSlopeSe$.

\subsection{The recurrence statistic under the null}
\label{sec:nullscatter}

The bound in the crossing ledger is one formula. Writing $S$ and
$\sigma_{\rm d}$ for the discovery amplitude and its error, and $\bar{a}$ and
$\bar{\sigma}=(\sum_i\sigma_i^{-2})^{-1/2}$ for the inverse-variance
combination of the repeat readings at the predicted channel,
\begin{equation}
\sigma_{\rm excl}=\frac{S-\bar{a}}
{\sqrt{\sigma_{\rm d}^{2}+\bar{\sigma}^{2}}}
=\frac{T_{\rm pers}-T_{\rm rep}}{\sqrt{1+(T_{\rm pers}/T_\star)^{2}}},
\label{eq:excl}
\end{equation}
with $T_{\rm pers}=S/\bar{\sigma}$ and $T_{\rm rep}=\bar{a}/\bar{\sigma}$, so
every bound follows from Eq.~\ref{eq:excl} and three columns of that table.
$T_{\rm rep}$ is one cell at the carried drift; the maximum over every drift
at that cell, tabulated beside it, is never compared with $T_{\rm pers}$,
being positive on average whatever is there, and as an amplitude selected
from $10^{\TuCellsDecLo}$ to $10^{\TuCellsDecHi}$ cells at $T_\star\simeq5$ is
biased high, the limit is stated as $(T_{\rm rep}+5)/T_{\rm pers}$. The reading
is centred on zero, scattering by \NuNullSd{} about \NuNullMean{} over
\NuNullN{} single-cell readings of every repeat block in the census and
hold-out, less the \NuNullNRecExcl{} of the crossing that recurs, a molecular line. The bound's power is $\Phi(T_{\rm pers}-5)$ at the discovery
amplitude and $\Phi(5T_{\rm pers}/T_\star-5)$ at the trigger amplitude
$5S/T_\star$, the faintest that could have produced the crossing, and the
\HjNPowerLo{} where these are smallest are reported as untested; each
crossing's measured recovery chance accompanies this paper as data.
