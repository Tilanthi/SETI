\begin{abstract}
We search \NWinA{} fine-channel ALMA windows toward \NSysClassA{} systems
within 40\,pc, covering \CvUnionA\,GHz of union bandwidth in
\CovNIslands{} disjoint intervals, for narrowband drifting carriers;
\NWinB{} coarser windows constrain only an unresolved excess. Injection
gives a median single-epoch sensitivity, at ninety per cent recovery, of
$\EirpNinetySysMedA$\,W equivalent isotropic power for a carrier narrower
than the instrumental channel. We find \EvNCrossRaw{} threshold crossings
(channels and drift rates where the star reaches \StageNullTrig{} times the
scatter at control positions), \EvNCrossFlag{} in one block failing the
quality criterion. Of the other \EvNCrossQp{}, \MkNCoinc{} coincide with
catalogued molecular transitions, \LnAttrSupp{} with corroborating evidence
of line emission. The \CeObs{} unattributed crossings of the primary census
are consistent with chance against the \CeExp{} expected at control
positions ($P=\CeProbNull$), which does not exclude a smaller added
population; a narrower field gives \FrNarrowObs{} against \FrNarrowExp{}
($P=\FrNarrowProb$ before correcting for that choice). No confirmed carrier
is found. Coverage and the criterion, not depth, limit confirmation:
\ZcNoRepSys{} systems have no repeat at a covered frequency, and only a
carrier returning to its discovery frequency could be confirmed.
\end{abstract}

\keywords{Technosignatures, Search for extraterrestrial intelligence, Submillimeter astronomy, Radio spectroscopy, Debris disks}
