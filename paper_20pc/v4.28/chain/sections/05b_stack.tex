\subsection{A stellar-frame multi-epoch stacked search}
\label{sec:stack}

Combining the epochs of a star in that star's own rest frame deepens the
limit. \EpMulti{} of the \NSystems{} surveyed systems hold more than one
searched execution block. \SxStkStarCensus{} of the surveyed stars can be
stacked, \SttNStkOneEb{} of them from blocks beyond the census. Over
\SyStkNGroup{} stacked groups, \SyStkNEpochComb{} epoch-windows and
\SyStkHours\,h the flux-density limit improves on each star's best single epoch
by a median factor \SyStkGainMed{} and by \SyStkGainMax{} at most. A further
\SxStkStarOutside{} stars, all control stars beyond 40\,pc, are stacked and
excluded from those figures. \emph{A fixed stellar-frame frequency is not a
fixed observed frequency, so the epochs are registered before they are added}.
The barycentric term alone swings by a median \StkVcorrSwingMed\,km\,s$^{-1}$
between the epochs of one star, many channels at the \StkRefChanKHz\,kHz
Class~A width. Each epoch is therefore transformed to the stellar rest frame,
divided by its own primary-beam response and combined by inverse variance over
the channels of common stellar-frame coverage, keyed on the star's catalogued
identity. The transform is verified on a real line, \StkPersistLineStar's,
which \StkRegNEpoch{} epochs in \StkRegSpanD\,d observe
\StkRegTopoKms\,km\,s$^{-1}$ -- \StkRegTopoChan{} channels -- apart and all
place at one stellar-frame frequency to \FrEpochStelKms\,km\,s$^{-1}$. Every stacked limit is
measured by injection into the stack itself and never read off a stacked
spectrum as a threshold. The criterion is evaluated identically at the stacked
control positions, so that the whole test's false-alarm rate is measured.
\emph{The gain is $\sqrt{N_{\rm eff}}$ and not
$\sqrt{N}$}, where $N_{\rm eff}=\sigma_{\min}^{2}\sum_e\sigma_e^{-2}$ is the
equivalent number of equal-depth epochs. The median $N_{\rm eff}/N$ is
\StkNeffFrac{} and the realised gain against $\sqrt{N}$ is \StkGainVsN. The
shortfall is unequal epoch depth, so an epoch count alone misleads.

\emph{The deepest stacked limit is Proxima Centauri's, and it is a Class~B
limit.} Its \SxProxEbCensus{} census blocks are all coarse \ChanBModeMHz\,MHz
windows. The stack therefore constrains an unresolved spectral excess,
discriminates no drift and reaches $\mathrm{EIRP}_{90}=\SzProxEirpCensus$\,W.
\emph{Every stacked limit holds only for an emitter steady in the stellar frame
to within one of its own channels, and \PxPlanet{} does not satisfy that.} At
\PxPorbD\,d about a star of \PxMassMsun\,M$_\odot$ the planet moves at
\PxVorbKms\,km\,s$^{-1}$. These are the archive's default parameter set
\citep{NEA2013}, for the planet of \citet{AngladaEscude2016} and a host mass
agreeing with \citet{Kervella2017}. A transmitter on the planet therefore
swings over \PxPPKms$\sin i$\,km\,s$^{-1}$, some \PxNChanWalk{} channels at
$\sin i=1$. The stack cancels such a carrier instead of building it unless the
orbit is seen nearly face-on ($\sin i\le\PxSiniMax$, \PxIsoPct{} per cent of
randomly oriented orbits). Within a single block the same transmitter gains at most
\PxBlockDvKms\,km\,s$^{-1}$, \PxBlockPct{} per cent of one channel. For that reason the
single-epoch limit of $\PxEirpSingle$\,W -- the deepest single epoch in the
archive, from a reserved block and not a census limit -- is the one that applies
to it, and the deepest limit this survey places on the nearest plausible
transmitter site. The same tolerance
divides the sample. A fine-channel stack admits a far smaller stellar-frame
excursion than a coarse one, so the coarse stacks buy reflex tolerance with
bandwidth. No crossing of \S\ref{sec:etacrv} drifts slowly enough to survive a
fine-channel stack, and the single-epoch limits of \S\ref{sec:excludes} are the
ones that apply generally. \emph{\StkNSurvive{} groups pass the full criterion
and both are known lines}: $\beta$~Pictoris carbon monoxide in two transitions.
That is the astrophysical positive control of \S\ref{sec:bpicval}, passing in
the stack as well. The survey's own $\pm\StkAttrKms$\,km\,s$^{-1}$ mask leaves
\StkUnattrClause.
