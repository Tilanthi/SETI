\subsection{Frequency frames, linewidth and smearing}
%% (subsection label retired: nothing cites it)

Every frequency axis here is \emph{topocentric}, as the correlator delivers
it; velocities enter only at vetting, where the line mask carries each
crossing to the stellar frame (Appendix~\ref{app:linemask}). The native
channel width is the linewidth convention for every threshold:
EIRP$_{\rm trig}$ is the minimum \emph{total} power of a channel-confined
carrier, never a spectral power density, and spreading it over $N$ channels
lowers the statistic as $N^{\ScNBroadExp}$, so a feature as wide as the widest
line these fields resolve (\ScNBroadChan{} channels) must carry
\ScNBroadRatio$\times$ a carrier's power. The Hz-resolution surveys' limits
are total powers in the same sense, which makes the axes of
Fig.~\ref{fig:context}(a) commensurable.

ALMA applies online Hanning smoothing by default \citep{ALMAHandbook}, so
neighbouring channels are correlated: for the $0.25/0.5/0.25$ kernel
$\rho_1=\HanRhoOneFrac=\HanRhoOne$, $\rho_2=\HanRhoTwo$ and
$\sigma_{\rm smoothed}/\sigma_{\rm raw}=\HanSigRatio$, and the peak
channel holds \HanBest{} of an unresolved carrier's flux at a channel centre
and \HanWorst{} on a boundary, a median \HanMed{} over sub-channel phase.
\emph{That loss enters the limits exactly once; the thresholds are free of it
and the limits are not.} A window's trigger flux is the bare
$\LtCommonSnr\sigma$ of its own spectrum, so the \emph{total} power of a
channel-confined carrier set against it would be optimistic by
$\times\HanFacBest$--$\times\HanFacWorst$. The injected carriers are not set
against it in that way: each is deposited with the correlator's own channel
response, at a sub-channel phase drawn afresh, and recovered through the same
trigger, which reads the peak channel. The measured completeness therefore
already contains the loss, and factorises into it exactly:
$\EirpNinetyMultA\,P_{\rm trig}$ is $\times\RpRespFac$ for the channel
response and $\times\RpMarginNinety$ for the margin a peak channel needs
above the nominal trigger for nine carriers in ten to return. So
$P_{\rm trig}$, the sensitivity $S_{\min}$ below and the filled symbols of
Fig.~\ref{fig:context}(a) are nominal throughout, which is what makes them
commensurable with other programmes' detection thresholds, while
$\mathrm{EIRP}_{90}$ carries the correction once.
That factor is $1/\InjDepMean$, the phase-mean peak-channel share a deposited
tone keeps, \InjShortPct{} per cent short of the exact response's
\InjRespMean{}, so the completeness is conservative by that much.

\emph{Intra-integration smearing, which the injections do not model,} is
divided out of every limit once as $\eta_{\rm smear}$, the mean channel
response over the sweep within one integration; it exceeds one per cent in
\NmRespNApplied, reaching $\times\SmcFacWorst$, and is measured for
\SmcNMeas{} of the \NWindows{} windows and bounded for the other \SmcNBound{}
at \SmcDtCeil\,s, the longest integration delivered. Re-scoring the
\SmcValNWin{} injected windows with every tone de-rated by its own
$\eta_{\rm smear}$ gives a penalty never larger than the one applied, so the
correction errs the safe way; the full treatment is in
\texttt{documentation/\allowbreak appendix\_A\_smearing.md} of the data archive
\citep{SIdeposit}.

\subsection{The primary-beam response}
\label{app:pbresp}

The sensitivity quoted for a window is $S_{\min}=5\sigma/A$, where $A$ is the
primary-beam response at the star's offset from the phase centre. Archival
fields are pointed at catalogue positions, so these high-proper-motion stars
can sit well off axis -- a median \PbOffMed$''$ and at most \BmOffMax$''$
over the \PbNWin{} windows, $A$ falling to \BmRespMin{} and below $0.9$ on
\BmNBelowNine{} -- each a J2000 pointing on a fast-moving star rather than an
error.

\emph{The response applied is a Gaussian of the full width the observatory
documents}, $\RingPbCoef\,\lambda/D$ \citep{ALMAHandbook}, at each window's
own dish diameter. It matches the blocked-aperture response \textsc{casa}
applies \citep{CASA2022} to \BmDocAiryPct{} per cent in width; the two part
company with offset, which sets the retention rule of \S\ref{sec:sample}, and
\emph{the furthest census window lies at $\BmCensusWorstC\,\lambda/D$, where
they agree to \BmCensusWorstPct{} per cent, the largest beam-model uncertainty
any sensitivity here carries.}



\emph{The correction is applied once}: $S_{\min}=5\sigma/A$ closes on all
\PbSminClosed{} catalogued rows to \PbSminWorstRes, and to \PbSminIndepRes{}
on the \PbSminIndep{} whose $A$ is not back-solved from $S_{\min}$, while the
statistic, ranks and attribution are ratios within one window, in which $A$
divides out.

\emph{The annual parallax is measured rather than modelled}: a carrier
injected at the full apparent position of the \SensNReextWin{} furthest-out
windows and recovered from the same visibilities returns the normalised dirty
beam, \SensPxSmallMeas{} against a predicted \SensPxSmallPred{} at
\SensPxSmallBeams{} of a beam and \SensPxLargeMeas{} against
\SensPxLargePred{} at \SensPxLargeBeams, so a limit corrected by the model is
shallower than it need be. $\epsilon$\,Eridani, with \BmEpsNOff{} of its
\BmEpsNWin{} windows $\BmEpsCLo$--$\BmEpsCHi\,\lambda/D$ off axis and the
largest parallax in the search, has no parallax record, so its limit is
optimistic by at most \BmEpsPxBoundPct{} per cent.

\emph{A component of a bound pair does not travel in a straight line, and the
propagation assumes it does.} \OmNComponent{} of the \OmNStar{} searched
positions have a companion within \OmRadius{} arcsec \citep{wds,orb6}, the
displacement taken from a visual orbit or bounded from the separation, with
masses from \citet{pecaut2013}, or from the proper-motion anomaly
\citep{kervella2022}; the largest is \OmWorstArcsec{} arcsec at \OmWorstStar,
\OmWorstBeams{} of a beam, and no system's limit moves by more than
\OmSysMovedMaxPct{} per cent. The exception is \OmFlagStars, whose solutions
are disturbed with no companion and no Hipparcos epoch, so the \OmNWinFlag{}
windows of those \OmNFlag{} stars carry limits optimistic by an unmeasured
amount.
