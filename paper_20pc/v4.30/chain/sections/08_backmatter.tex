\section*{Data availability}
\label{sec:data}
Every observation used here is public in the ALMA Science Archive under the
\NProjCodes{} project codes of Table~\ref{tab:projcodes}, and is retrieved by
the execution-block identifier, which the crossing ledger gives for each of the
\EvNCrossRaw{} threshold crossings \citep{Chen2022}; the astrometry is
Gaia~DR3, the planet counts the NASA Exoplanet Archive, and the line mask one
Splatalogue query of CDMS and JPL made on \McQueryDate. The machine-readable
data and the software are archived on Zenodo \citep{SIdeposit}. The principal
results are reproduced from the per-window catalogue of all \NWindows{}
windows, the Class~A and Class~B sensitivity measurements, the crossing
ledger, the control-field statistics and the execution-block accounting
(every row of Table~\ref{tab:counts} and Table~\ref{tab:blockledger}): from
these tables the counts, the chance expectation of \CeExp{} crossings, the
$\mathrm{EIRP}_{90}$ summaries and the line-recurrence bounds rebuild row by
row, and its programs rebuild the probability of the observed count, the
completeness factor with its intervals, the sensitivity summaries and the
widened-window recurrence comparison from the deposited resampling inputs,
injection trials, covering windows and seeds; the principal tables
also accompany the arXiv version of this paper as ancillary files. The archive
also holds the search pipeline as it ran and short reference implementations
of Equation~\ref{eq:tstar}, the control positions, the injection harness and
the line mask. The dynamic spectra at the star
and at its \RcNRetCtrl{} retained control positions, around each of the
\DaNSpecCross{} crossings, form a second file of the same record
(\DaSpecFileMB\,MB). Not
public are the calibrated visibilities, of order a terabyte for the \NEB{}
blocks, and the per-window spectra, which were discarded as the search
consumed them for lack of storage and not to restrict access; this execution
of the search therefore cannot be repeated, only run again from the archive
with the same code. Some windows in blocks that were not recalibrated carry products made
before the extractor reached its deposited form, which places the phase
centre at the star's parallax-displaced position and keeps every field
entry (the first form also drew its control annulus differently). Run
again on freshly calibrated 7\,m and 12\,m blocks, the code that made them
reproduces their search products in every window tested; the deposited
extractor changes the statistic more on the 12\,m array than on the 7\,m
array, through the parallax term.
