R2-3 REVERSES, and it is the most important change here (D32). The Re/sigma--T* relation is
now measured over 2,141 recovered Class A tones and it does the opposite of what the paper
concluded. The two estimators DO track for a real source at the star: the median Re/sigma per
unit T* stays between 1.06 and 1.17 in all 7 recovered-T* bins over a factor of eight in
amplitude, so the failure to track across the crossings is evidence about the crossings. At
the events' own T* bin injected sources give 6.58 +- 1.97 over 283 fits, 0.986 reaching
Re/sigma >= 4 and 0.977 passing all three clauses -- the power the paper had asserted without
measuring. Applied to the events the relation does not exclude them: 30 of the 33 unattributed
fitted crossings in the bin localise, the six deepest per star return +4.86 to +5.26 spanning
percentiles 9-23, and the rank-flagged one, 61 Vir, sits at the 23rd. "Excluded at
3.3-6.1 sigma" was not merely unsupported, it was backwards. The superseded-epoch values it
rested on are not comparable: re-fitted at that epoch the same real sources return a median
Re/sigma of 1.52 and a 16th percentile of -0.49, and 61 Vir's own superseded value is -0.93 --
the referee's number, read out of the released ledger's own record of it. Both falsifiers are
driven, and every transcribed percentile is asserted against the deposit's own 16th percentile
and against the ledger's Re/sigma.

And the structural reason the test cannot arbitrate authenticity is now stated outright: the
trigger and the fit are not independent measurements. A noise maximum AT the star has flux at
the star, so it too returns Re/sigma ~ T* ~ 5; the fit's power lies entirely in the two clauses
that test displacement, and those reject almost nothing (|Im/sigma| < 3 for 0.992 of injected
tones there). Nor is a 5 sigma crossing in a Class A window surprising: in the campaign's own
uninjected passes 11 of 12 Class A windows already contain a CONTROL position reaching T = 5,
median control maximum 5.99, against 1 of 28 for Class B. The discriminating evidence is the
rank screen, recurrence and the stellar-frame line mask.
