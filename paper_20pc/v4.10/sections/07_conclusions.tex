\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusions}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em,itemsep=4pt,topsep=4pt,parsep=0pt]

\item ALMA's public archive opens a region of the technosignature search
space that had not been examined toward nearby stars. Of the
\UnionGHz\,GHz of unique sky frequency searched here, only
\UnionBandThree\,GHz lies inside the frequency interval covered by
centimetre-wave programmes.

\item The primary experiment is \NWinA{} fine-channel spectral windows
toward \NSysClassA{} stellar systems, covering \DnuA\,GHz of unique
frequency, searched for an unresolved carrier whose frequency drifts. A
secondary coarse-channel spectral-excess search covers a further
\DnuBonly\,GHz in \NWinB{} windows but cannot discriminate drift, and both
come from one uniform reduction of \LedProcessed{} public execution blocks,
\TotalDataTB\,TB of raw data.

\item The sensitivity is measured by injection and recovery through the
complete automated selection rather than inferred from a threshold:
$\mathrm{EIRP}_{90}=\EirpNinetyMultA\,P_{\rm trig}$, a median of
$\EirpNinetySysMedA$\,W per system. That is deep enough for
\BenchNSysA{} of the \NSysClassA{} Class~A systems to exclude a
\BenchDiam\,m dish radiating \BenchPowerMW\,MW at \BenchFreqGHz\,GHz, whose
EIRP is $\BenchEirp$\,W.

\item \NCross{} threshold crossings are reported with their dispositions,
and \NAttributed{} are attributable to catalogued molecular transitions,
almost all of them circumstellar or foreground carbon monoxide, among them
the $\beta$~Pictoris debris disc recovered blind in two transitions. None of the remaining \NUnattributed{} is independently
confirmed: none recurs in the repeat observations available, and
\NUnattrRankFlagged{} also outrank all \NCtrl{} spatial controls.

\item Combining the epochs of each star in that star's rest frame deepens
the flux limit by a median factor \StkLimitGainMed{} over the best single
epoch. For Proxima~Centauri the stacked limit, measured by injection into the
stack, is $\StkProxEirpCal$\,W for an emitter steady in the stellar frame to
\StkProxTolKms\,km\,s$^{-1}$ across epochs spanning \StkProxSpanYr\,yr.

\item These results constrain persistent, unresolved carriers within the
frequency, drift, duty-cycle and sensitivity domain actually searched,
toward the stars ALMA happened to observe. They cannot be converted into an
occurrence rate for transmitters in the 40\,pc stellar population, which
would require a denominator an archive-selected sample does not supply.

\end{enumerate}
