\section*{Data Availability}
\label{sec:data}
The release carries every reduced data product, the full
target-selection and analysis pipeline, the \NWindows-row per-window
catalogue, the injection records and the control statistics. Deposited
beside the catalogue are the per-window search statistics, one row each,
and the frozen inputs of every derived quantity in this paper: the
archival harvest, the matched recurrence fits, the frequency-clustering
computation, the block-by-block archival verdicts, and the ALMA delivery
record for every block in scope that could not be searched. What has been
demonstrated rather than claimed is narrower, and it is a measured number
in both cases: \NRegenProducts{} generated products regenerate
byte-identically from the frozen inputs, and \NReproChecks{} headline
quantities are re-derived from the released catalogue alone, without the
pipeline. \emph{The reference of record is the Zenodo deposit}, which is
frozen, carries a DOI minted on acceptance, and is the version any
reproduction should cite; the repository at
\url{https://github.com/Tilanthi/SETI} is a working mirror and may move on.
