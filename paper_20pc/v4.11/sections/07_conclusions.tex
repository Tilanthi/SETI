\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusions}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em,itemsep=4pt,topsep=4pt,parsep=0pt]

\item ALMA's public archive opens a region of search space unexamined
toward nearby stars. No search above about 30\,GHz has reached a
star within 40\,pc, and the one previous Band~3 search targeted stars at
\MasonNearKpc\,kpc and beyond. Of the \UnionGHz\,GHz of unique sky
frequency searched here in either class, \UnionBandThree\,GHz lies inside
the frequency axis of the \citet{Wright2018} search haystack, which defines a parameter
space and not coverage by any telescope.

\item The primary experiment is \NWinA{} fine-channel windows toward
\NSysClassA{} stellar systems, covering \DnuA\,GHz of unique frequency in
disconnected intervals, searched for an unresolved carrier whose frequency
drifts. A secondary coarse-channel search for an unresolved spectral excess
adds \DnuBonly\,GHz in \NWinB{} windows, with no drift discrimination. Both
come from one uniform reduction of \NEB{} public execution blocks,
\TotalDataTB\,TB of raw data.

\item Sensitivity is a measured completeness, not a threshold. Carriers
injected into the real calibrated visibilities are recovered through the
unchanged selection, the spatial ranking included, and
$\mathrm{EIRP}_{90}$ is the power at which nine in ten come back. It is
quoted per window: $\StrMultNoise\,P_{\rm trig}$ where the control ring is
noise, a bound where resolved disc emission raises it. The median system's
best window reaches $\EirpNinetySysMedA$\,W, with a spread across systems
of $\EirpNinetySysLoA$--$\EirpNinetySysHiA$\,W.

\item All \NCross{} threshold crossings are reported with their
dispositions. Of these, \NAttributed{} coincide with a catalogued molecular
transition, almost all of them carbon monoxide. Of the remaining
\NUnattributed{}, \RcNTested{}
were tested for recurrence in the star's own frame, at every later
observation covering the event frequency: none is present again, the largest
statistic at any predicted cell being \RcTMax{} against
$T_{\rm trig}=\StageNullTrig$, and the weakest exclusion \RcExclMin$\sigma$.
The one crossing not tested had not retained its discovery spectrum, so
there was no amplitude to carry into the epochs that do cover it. Exactly
\NUnattrRankFlagged{} of them
outranks all \NCtrl{} control positions, which is a prioritisation and not
a confirmation.

\item What this survey could search and what it could confirm are different
extents and must not be read as one statement. The carrier search covered
\RcSearchSys{} systems with fine-channel coverage, of which only
\RcConfSysDay{}, carrying
\RcConfPctDay{} per cent of the fine-channel bandwidth counted window by
window, were observed again at the same frequency more than a day apart,
and \RcConfSysYr{} beyond a year. A transmitter is persistent
here if it would be detectable above the quoted power in every independent
observation covering its frequency.

\item Combining each star's epochs in its own rest frame deepens the flux
limit by a median factor \StkLimitGainMed. For Proxima~Centauri the stacked
limit, measured by injection into the stack, is \StkProxGain$\times$ deeper
than that star's own best single epoch and reaches $\StkProxEirpCal$\,W in
\ChanBModeMHz\,MHz channels --- a Class~B limit on an unresolved excess,
carrying no drift discrimination --- for an emitter steady in the stellar
frame to \StkProxTolKms\,km\,s$^{-1}$ across \StkProxSpanYr\,yr.

\item These results constrain persistent, unresolved carriers within the
frequency, drift, duty-cycle and sensitivity domain actually searched,
toward the stars ALMA happened to observe, and not the solar neighbourhood.
The more transferable result is what such a search runs into. Thermal
sensitivity does not bind it --- ALMA supplies more than the experiment can
use --- but molecular-line confusion, a channelisation chosen by other
observers for other purposes, and how often the archive happened to look
twice.

\end{enumerate}
