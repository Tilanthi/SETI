paper v4.17: a calibration exclusion withdrawn and replaced by a measurement; the primary beam correctly attributed

The amplitude argument against a channel-dependent calibration residual is
withdrawn. It was built on each star's photospheric continuum; measured
instead on the continuum the pipeline records at the extraction position,
with dust inside the beam, that estimate was low by a median factor of 651
and by 1.3e4 at one star. Nine windows toward two stars admit the mechanism
at 4-13 per cent, inside the documented 5-20 per cent range. The claim is
deleted and replaced by a stronger, empirical one: in all nine the largest
statistic anywhere is 3.1 against a trigger of 5 and none holds a crossing,
bounding the coherent residual below 7 per cent, and below 2 per cent at the
star whose continuum is best measured. The mechanism is admissible and is
measured not to be operating. CD-57 1054 carries the argument within one
star: six of its windows admit 9-13 per cent and do nothing, while its one
crossing sits in a window that would need 71 per cent.

Appendix A.2 named the wrong primary-beam model as the one its own limits
were computed with. The applied response is a Gaussian of the Airy
first-null width, 1.22 lambda/D, reproduced from the released attenuation
column on all 1651 windows to three parts in a million -- not the 1.13
lambda/D the appendix claimed to have adopted. Nothing moves, because the
effect is 1e-5 in the median window, but the attribution flips and the
signed bias is now stated and measured: 0.02 per cent in the median system,
above 10 per cent in four windows, worst 10.3 per cent at 8.1 arcsec off
axis. A value assertion had checked the column since round 11 and passed
every build; what no gate checked was whether the prose named the same
coefficient. It now does, and fails the build otherwise.

Recurrence power is quoted at the trigger amplitude rather than the
discovery amplitude: 26 of 33 crossings are excluded above 90 per cent and
seven are untested rather than excluded, among them one of the two events
that lead their own control fields. The beta Pictoris pair is excluded by
the criterion applied to every other pair, by the narrowest margin in the
set. The chance comparison is stated as compatibility with the empirical
control expectation, 32 observed against 28.9 expected, and the reserved
hold-out as compatibility with that expectation rather than as the shape of
the tail. Confirmable systems are 37 and unconfirmable 23, replacing 39 and
21 in six sentences. The attribution rule is the stellar frame with the
local standard of rest as a check, stated once; the HD 48370 coincidence is
foreground gas on measurement, tracking a cloud line rather than the star.
Two false novelty claims are removed and a third corrected against the page
images of both earlier searches: each covered a single line, over no more
than 35 MHz, with no test of persistence.
