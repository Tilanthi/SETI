\begin{abstract}
We search ALMA archives for narrowband drifting carriers from \NSysClassA{}
systems within 40\,pc: \NWinA{} fine-channel windows, \EdrNWinA{} resolving
a drift, \CvUnionA\,GHz of union bandwidth in \CovNIslands{} disjoint
intervals, with \NWinB{} coarser windows constraining only an unresolved
excess. Spectra are de-drifted to $\DriftCeilLo$--$\DriftCeilHi$\,nHz,
\AccelSheikhPct{} per cent of the recommended ceiling; a crossing is one
channel and drift rate at \StageNullTrig{} times the scatter at control
positions. The median system's best window triggers on
$\EirpNinetySysMedA$\,W nine times in ten by injection, for a carrier
narrower than the instrumental channel.
No confirmed carrier is found. Of the crossings, \MkNCoinc{} coincide with
catalogued molecular transitions, \LnAttrSupp{} with corroborating evidence:
line emission explains the strongest. Unattributed crossings -- \CeObs{} of
the primary census -- are consistent with chance against the \CeExp{}
expected at control positions, which does not exclude a smaller added
population. No crossing returns to the stellar-frame frequency its own
discovery predicts, which is the only recurrence this reading tests.
Coverage, not depth, limits confirmation: \ZcNoRepSys{} systems have no
repeat at a covered frequency.
\end{abstract}

\keywords{Technosignatures, Search for extraterrestrial intelligence, Submillimeter astronomy, Radio spectroscopy, Debris disks}
