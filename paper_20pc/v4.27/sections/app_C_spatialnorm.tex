\section{The spatial rank, and what it can support}
\label{app:radius}

The spatial rank asks whether an excess sits at the star or anywhere else in
the field. It is a diagnostic, no disposition rests on it, and this appendix
is the measurement that settles why: \rankstatus{}

The star lies at the phase centre, inside the control annulus, and the
displacement is real: the star carries more above-threshold cells than a
control position at every level below the trigger. The chance expectation of
\S\ref{sec:trigunif} is not stepped by it, since the ratio at the trigger is
formed from the cells under test, and what carrying it in would do is measured
in Appendix~\ref{app:falsealarm}. \emph{It is not a primary-beam effect.}
Spectra are extracted from phase-rotated visibilities in apparent flux density
and are never corrected for the primary-beam response
(Appendix~\ref{app:conventions}), so the familiar rise of image noise with
distance from the pointing centre cannot operate here; were it operating it
would make the control probes noisier than the star and place the stellar rank
\emph{above} one half, the opposite of what is measured. The control ensemble
does show a weak rise of its own level with radius, and each probe is
therefore standardised within its own radius bin, with no free parameter.

\emph{A calibration error that varied with frequency would explain the
displacement, and it is the only mechanism on offer.} An error identical in
every channel is removed exactly by the per-integration median along
frequency; a channel-dependent one is not, and two ordinary errors are
channel-dependent: a residual in the bandpass, and a system temperature
measured in the coarse correlator mode and interpolated onto the science
channels, which near a telluric line leaves a narrow feature behind
\citep{ALMATsys2018}. Both multiply the continuum, so both act at the star and
not on empty sky, both stand still in topocentric frequency and both are
coherent between integrations: they would stack at near-zero drift and put the
stellar rank \emph{below} one half, the sign observed.
\emph{Amplitude excludes them, measured where the search looks.} Such an
error multiplies the continuum at the extraction position and must beat the
noise of the de-drifted, block-combined spectrum, since a coherent residual
stacks as a carrier does, so a fractional error $\epsilon$ depositing
$\epsilon S_{\rm c}$ at the star needs $\epsilon=5\sigma/S_{\rm c}$ to reach
the trigger. That continuum is measured for each window in its own execution
block, as the time average of the window's own retained spectra, over
\CyNWinMeas{} of the \CyNWinTot{} windows, and over \CyNRouteBlock{} blocks it
agrees with independent continuum images of the same fields with no offset
between the two (\CyRouteBiasMJy\,mJy). No window requires as little as the
\CySpecTsysLo--\CySpecTsysHi{} per cent a coarse system temperature is
documented to leave at an ozone line -- the smallest requirement anywhere is
\CyEpsMinPct{} per cent and the median \CyEpsMedPct, and over the
\CyNCrossMeas{} crossings with a measured continuum the least demanding needs
\CyCrossEpsLoPct{} per cent -- while the bandpass branch fails by amplitude
everywhere, needing $\CyMarginBp\times$ the \CdSpecBpWorst{} per cent
variation the observatory quotes for its worst bands
\citep{ALMAKBBandpass,ALMATechNote15}; and of either sample \ScNInRad{}
crossing lies within \ScRadMHz\,MHz, two of the \CdSpecTsysRes\,MHz channels
on which the system temperature is measured, of a catalogued atmospheric
transition (Appendix~\ref{app:rfi}). The shared noise scale raises one further
question, and the same arithmetic settles it: over the \NsrNWin{} of the
\NWindows{} census windows holding none of the adopted crossings, the stellar
statistic divided by the median of the \NCtrl{} control statistics is exactly
the ratio of the two noises, its median \NsrMed{} (\NsrLo--\NsrHi), so the two
scales agree.

\subsection{The reserved hold-out, and what it calibrates}
\label{sec:heldout}

The statistic and the mask are set on the searched sample and cannot also
calibrate themselves, so a further sample is separated by an identifier rule:
a block is held out if and only if
$\mathrm{sha256}(\textsc{uid})_{[:8]} \bmod 5 = 0$, subject to two guards that
use identifiers alone -- a block on a named list stays in the
survey, and no system loses every block. That this sample is independent of
the choice of statistic and of mask is an assertion of ours and not something
a reader can check. Of \NEbProcessedAll{} blocks processed, \NEbHeldOut{} are
reserved, \HoPctHeld{} per cent of them, carrying \HoWin{} windows toward
\HoStars{} stars, with no star and no system lost from the headline sample.
The rule hashes the execution block, and ALMA's identifier carries the
observing session above it, so \HsSharePct{} per cent of the reserved set --
\BkSessHoBlocks{} of the \NEbHeldOut{} blocks, across \BkSessShared{} sessions
-- shares calibrators, weather and correlator setup with a searched block,
where a session-level hash would have been the stronger choice; every quantity
drawn from this sample is therefore given twice, over the whole reserved set
and over the \HsIndBlocks{} blocks that share no session with it. One further
quantity is calibrated on those blocks and on nothing else, the radial
behaviour of the control ensemble: the standardised level runs \HoRadBins{}
across the annulus, a mean per-window slope of
$\HoRadSlope\pm\HoRadSlopeSe$, small and of the wrong sign to account for the
displacement.

\subsection{The recurrence statistic under the null}
\label{sec:nullscatter}

The bound in the crossing ledger is one formula. Writing $S$ and
$\sigma_{\rm d}$ for the discovery amplitude and its error, and $\bar{a}$ and
$\bar{\sigma}=(\sum_i\sigma_i^{-2})^{-1/2}$ for the inverse-variance
combination of the repeat readings at the predicted channel,
\begin{equation}
\sigma_{\rm excl}=\frac{S-\bar{a}}
{\sqrt{\sigma_{\rm d}^{2}+\bar{\sigma}^{2}}}
=\frac{T_{\rm pers}-T_{\rm rep}}{\sqrt{1+(T_{\rm pers}/T_\star)^{2}}},
\label{eq:excl}
\end{equation}
with $T_{\rm pers}=S/\bar{\sigma}$ and $T_{\rm rep}=\bar{a}/\bar{\sigma}$, so
that every bound follows from Eq.~\ref{eq:excl} and three columns of that
table and nothing else: $T_{\rm rep}$ is read at the carried drift and at one
cell, and the largest value over every drift trial at the same cell is
tabulated beside it but is never compared with $T_{\rm pers}$, since a maximum
is positive on average whatever is there; an amplitude selected as the largest
of $10^{\TuCellsDecLo}$ to $10^{\TuCellsDecHi}$ cells at $T_\star\simeq5$ is
biased high, so the limit $(T_{\rm rep}+5)/T_{\rm pers}$ is the form of the
statement that survives the bias; the reading itself is centred on zero and
scatters by \NuNullSd{} about a mean of \NuNullMean, measured over \NuNullN{}
single-cell readings of every repeat block in the census and the hold-out;
where the bound has power is $\Phi(T_{\rm pers}-5)$ for a carrier radiating
through both observations at the discovery amplitude and
$\Phi(5T_{\rm pers}/T_\star-5)$ at the trigger amplitude $5S/T_\star$, the
faintest that could have produced the crossing at all, so that where they are
small the crossing is untested and the \HjNPowerLo{} smallest are reported
that way; and each crossing's own recovery chance, calculated and measured by
injecting a carrier at the trigger amplitude at its predicted cell through the
instrument's own channel response, accompanies this paper as data.

control, over the same \BpicPanelNDrawn{} of the \NCross{} crossings, the
remainder retaining no control ring. In its second panel the
