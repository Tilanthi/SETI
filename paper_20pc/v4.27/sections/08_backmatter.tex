\emph{Testing and validation of that analysis.} No result here is accepted on a
model's assertion: every number in the text is written by the program that
computes it from the data rather than transcribed, which removes transcription

\section*{Data availability}
\label{sec:data}
\emph{Public.} Every observation this paper rests on is public and is
identified here precisely enough to be fetched: the \NEB{} primary-census
execution blocks, and those of every other set of
Table~\ref{tab:blockledger}, are held in the ALMA Science Archive under the
\NProjCodes{} project codes of Table~\ref{tab:projcodes} and are retrieved by
the execution-block identifier, which the crossing ledger gives for each of the
\EvNCrossRaw{} threshold crossings \citep{Chen2022}; the astrometry is
Gaia~DR3, the planet counts the NASA Exoplanet Archive, and the mask one
Splatalogue query of CDMS and JPL made on \McQueryDate{} and repeatable as it
stands.

\emph{Deposited.} The machine-readable data behind this paper are deposited as
a citable archive \citep{SIdeposit}: the per-window catalogue of all
\NWindows{} searched windows; the Class~A and Class~B sensitivity measurements;
the \EvNCrossRaw{} threshold crossings with their molecular identifications,
the atmospheric flag and both field readings of the trigger; the
repeat-observation readings; the injection trials and the completeness
calibration carried from them; the control-field statistics, including the
above-threshold cell counts below the trigger; the execution-block and count
accounting, all \MtCountRows{} rows of Table~\ref{tab:counts} included; and the
data behind the figures -- every table describing its own columns and units and
carrying the project code and execution block that tie each row to an
observation, five of them also accompanying the paper on the arXiv as ancillary
files. The archive includes short reference implementations of the four
definitions the results rest on -- the statistic of Equation~\ref{eq:tstar},
the control-position generator, the injection--recovery harness and the line
mask -- runnable against the deposited tables; they implement the published
definitions and are not the pipeline that produced the survey.

\emph{What the deposit supports.} With Table~\ref{tab:blockledger} for the
block and window partition and Appendix~\ref{app:persys} for every system of
the carrier search, it rebuilds the window, star and system counts, the
crossing counts and their classification, the chance expectation of \CeExp{}
crossings as a sum over each Class~A window's own control exceedance rate,
masked share and atmospheric factor, the $\mathrm{EIRP}_{90}$ summaries
including which windows carry an injected completeness and which a transferred
one, and every exclusion depth with its recovery probability. Two quantities it
does not give on their own: the interval on the chance expectation, whose
resampling draws on quantities that are not per-window, and the completeness of
the individually injected windows, whose injection was made into
per-integration spectra that are not deposited.

\emph{Not public.} The calibrated visibilities, the per-window products and the
code that produced them are not distributed, so this execution of the search
cannot be re-run; the published statistical summaries rebuild from the
deposited tables row by row, while whether the description here suffices to
implement the search afresh has not been tested.
