THE CENTRAL CHANGE (R1-1, R2-2). Both referees required that the
radius-corrected normalisation replace the originally frozen screen as the
primary statistic, on the grounds that the radial displacement is not a
post-hoc rationalisation but a characterised instrumental effect with a
known mechanism. They were right. Images are primary-beam corrected, so
the true noise rises with distance from the pointing centre, while the
search divided every position by one global scale; the control probes
therefore sat at systematically different effective noise from the star,
which is at the phase centre. The displacement is measured three times
independently -- pre-registered hold-out, radial profile of the control
ensemble, prospectively searched extension -- and pseudo-stars reproduce
it where no star is present.
