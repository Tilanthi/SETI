\section{The false-alarm expectation and the control ensemble}
\label{app:falsealarm}

\subsection{The expectation and its resampling}

One attribution set serves both sides of the comparison, the mask of
Appendix~\ref{app:linemask} being applied to a control maximum exactly as to a
crossing. Over the \CeNWin{} Class~A windows $w$ of the primary census the
expected number of unattributed crossings is
\begin{equation*}
\bar N=\tau\sum_{w}r_w\,(1-a_w),\qquad r_w=\frac{n_w}{N_{\rm ctrl}},
\end{equation*}
where $n_w$ of the window's $N_{\rm ctrl}=\NCtrl$ control positions reach the
trigger, $a_w$ is the share of control exceedances that land inside the
mask, and $\tau$ is the share the atmospheric screen leaves. The rates alone
give \CeExpTrig{} expected crossings. The attributed share is measured where control maxima
actually fall, on the \RcNRetCtrl{} positions per window that keep a dynamic
spectrum. Of their \CeNExc{} exceedances, \CeNExcAttr{} lie inside the
mask, so attribution takes \CeMaskPct{} per cent. The screen
(Appendix~\ref{app:rfi}) then removes \CeTlCtrlCut{} of the \CeTlCtrlSurv{}
that remain, leaving $\bar N=\CeExp$ against \CeObs{} observed. The
\CeNWinTail{} Class~A windows of the archival tail and the \CeNWinComp{} of
\OmXStar, a position the catalogue does not tabulate, enter neither side.
Near a band edge the pooled exceedance rate is $\times\VnEdgeRatio$ the
interior rate, but one window, toward HD~48370, holds \VnEdgeDomPct{} per
cent of the near-edge exceedances; without it the ratio is
$\times\VnEdgeNoDomRatio$ ($\times\VnEdgeHalfCleanRatio$ on a held-out half,
$\pv{\VnEdgeHalfCleanP}$), so no edge correction is applied.

Windows share execution blocks, so the count is compared through block
resampling. In each of $B=\CeNBoot$ resamplings the blocks are drawn with
replacement, each window's rate is redrawn binomially from its own probes,
and the attributed share and the screen are redrawn from their own counts,
giving $\bar N_b$; then
\begin{equation*}
P=\frac{1}{B}\sum_{b=1}^{B}\Pr\!\left(N\ge N_{\rm obs}\mid\bar N_b\right),
\end{equation*}
with $N$ a Poisson count and $N_{\rm obs}=\CeObs$, gives $P=\CeProbNull$,
conditional on the control-position model (Appendix~\ref{app:radius}). With
$k$ added to the mean $\bar N_b$ of every resampling, a count of \CeObs{} or
more keeps a probability of \CeProbAtLim{} for $k=\CeAddLim$, but for one
more it falls to \CeProbAboveLim, below the one-sided \CeAlphaPct{} per cent
level. So \CeAddLim{} is the largest additional mean number of primary crossings
compatible with the observed count, conditional on the mask and on the
control-position model, as quoted in \S\ref{sec:trigunif}. It is not a
confidence bound on a realised number of crossings. As a check, a pseudo-star read at one control position in every window of
a block is no more dispersed than one read window by window (variance ratio
\BgDisp) and reaches \CeObs{} with probability \BgPseudoP. The widened recurrence window
of \S\ref{sec:etacrv} is read in the same way over its \WuNWin{} distinct
windows.

\subsection{Execution-block quality}
\label{sec:blockquality}

The comparison assumes that each block's control ensemble behaves like
noise, and in one block all \NCtrl{} probes exceed the trigger. Every block
is therefore judged on the median of its windows' control ensembles over its
class median, which also flags \DqNWin{} of the \NWindows{} windows at
\BqFactor{} the survey median. The criterion was written after the condition
had been seen, but the choice is not a delicate one. The failing block sits
at \BqFailQ{} and the next at \BqNextQ, and its \BqFailNWin{} coarse-channel
windows hold no census crossing once ALMA's final flags are restored
(\S\ref{sec:method}).

\subsection{Registration}
\label{sec:regbudget}

Between epochs, the predicted cell is registered to \RgBudgetChMed{} of a
channel in every window. Widening each reading to the worst cell within
\RgTolKms\,km\,s$^{-1}$, re-anchoring it along the discovery track, or
raising the bar to \ZtTrialThr{} changes no disposition, although
registration to a single channel is tested only on the unresolved line of
\RgRegStar, which registers to \RgRegMaxCh{} of a channel.

\subsection{The pairs}
\label{sec:pairacct}

Where the epochs of a pair of crossings (\S\ref{sec:etacrv}) are close
enough for the predicted offset to be tighter than the searched window, all
\TjNPower{} such pairs fail a drift changing at a constant rate, by at least
\TjSigMin{} times the resolution. The $\beta$~Pictoris pair, whose crossings
lie \TjBpDvKms\,km\,s$^{-1}$ apart in the star's frame and \TjBpDtH\,h apart,
cannot be dismissed in this way. Its mean acceleration,
\TjBpAccelReq\,m\,s$^{-2}$, is inside the \WdAGrid\,m\,s$^{-2}$ ceiling, and
a circular orbit of \ObDayBp\,d about the \EcMassBp\,M$_\odot$ star
\citep{Crifo1997} reproduces it. Both blocks belong to one session and no
block of another session covers the window, so the pair is \TjBpVerdict.

\subsection{The drift grid's edges}
\label{sec:edge}

The drift trials run from $-\dot\nu_{\max}$ in half-channel steps to the
first trial at or beyond $+\dot\nu_{\max}$, so the searched interval is
asymmetric by up to one step. Of the \EgNDrift{} crossings of \EgDriftPop{}
with a fitted drift, the \EgNEdge{} toward \EgEdgeStars{} lie on an
outermost trial of their own window's grid. Their drift rates, in the
observatory frame, and the accelerations they imply are lower bounds, and
\EgNEdgeOut{} of them carry no amplitude bound.
