# The submission, checked — Open Journal of Astrophysics + arXiv

What this paper will actually be submitted against, requirement by requirement, with what is met
and what is not. OJA is an **arXiv overlay**: the published article *is* the arXiv PDF, there is no
typesetting and **no copy-editing at all**, so arXiv's rules bind as hard as the journal's and the
proofreading is entirely ours.

Every number below was **re-measured on the current build** of
`technosignatures_40pc_v4.18.tex` — **35 pp**, PDF md5 `e954d4778bae792f9a3e524062c1471d`,
measured 2026-10-13, between 06:40 and 07:30 UTC. Rebuild them all with, in order:

```
bash arxivset.sh                       # the submission set, checks A1-A12
python3 minuscheck.py                  # hyphen-for-minus, source and PDF (also in gate.sh)
python3 genai_sweep.py technosignatures_40pc_v4.18.pdf    # the GenAI-evidence sweep
python3 genai_sweep.py --drive         # 23 of 23 families must fire first
python3 abschars.py                    # arXiv's 1920 characters, on the typeset page
python3 abstract_limit.py              # the same limit by the independent source route
python3 absmeta.py                     # the plain-ASCII block in this file, which is pasted
python3 abswords.py                    # the word count -- ours, a style alarm, not a limit
```

The plain-ASCII abstract in §5 is regenerated by
`../../referee_r17/absascii_r17.py`, which reads `abschars.py --text` so that no value in it is
retyped by hand, and which now **refuses** to emit a block over 1920 characters rather than
printing the headroom and exiting zero. `absmeta.py`, in `gate.sh`, checks the block that is
actually in this file on every build: pure ASCII, within the limit, and **equal character for
character to the current typeset abstract transliterated**, so a value that moves in the paper and
not in the block fails the build instead of being submitted.

## 2. "No direct evidence of GenAI" — swept, on the typeset page

`genai_sweep.py` reads the **rendered PDF text** in three renderings — as extracted, with
end-of-line hyphenation rejoined, and with every hyphen replaced by a space — because a real hyphen
hid a sentence from a plain search, and rejoining without care invented two inconsistencies that
were not there. 23 pattern families: prompt words, second-person address, imperatives, "do not
re-run" handoff phrasing, agent and model names, round numbers, version strings, referee words,
build vocabulary, filenames, absolute paths, stray macro names, TODO markers, repository names,
promises of online-only material, and laboratory-notebook voice.

**Result: 9 hit lines across the 23 families, every one ordinary English or the declaration the
journal itself mandates**, and `--drive` fires **23 of 23** families on planted text first, so the
sweep is not passing vacuously:

- *"the one that **prompted** it"*, *"maximising over a plane of trajectories **lets** a carrier
  find a cell"*, and three sentences of the form *"the criterion **was fixed** before any stellar
  position was examined"* — all five are the paper's own plain prose;
- *"this particular execution of the search cannot be **re-run** by a reader"* — the data-
  availability statement saying what a reader cannot do, which is the opposite of a handoff note;
- *"Use of generative **artificial intelligence**"* and two uses of *"**assertion**"* — the
  mandated declaration and its validation account. These three hits exist **because** requirement
  1.4/1.5 is met.

Nothing else: no agent name, no round number, no version string, no filename, no `★`, no `%%`, no
code-shaped line, and no sentence promising a reader material that does not exist.

★ **The arXiv source bundle is served publicly too**, and the working tree's `.tex` files carry
agent names, referee items and first-person narration. `arxivset.sh` therefore copies **every
`.tex` comment-stripped**, and check A8 proves the strip changes not one byte of the output.

Also checked, each against arXiv's current page rather than from memory:
- **`\pdfoutput=1` is NOT added.** arXiv says plainly *"you should not use `\pdfoutput` to change
  the output format"* and detects pdflatex itself. The older advice said the opposite.
- **`xr`/`\externaldocument` is advised against outright** — file locations differ on their servers
  and cross-document links are lost. The paper used to load `xr` in its split build. It no longer
  does.
- **`\today` is advised against**, because arXiv occasionally rebuilds the PDF. `openjournal.cls`
  sets `\submitted{Version \today}`; the manuscript empties it, so no date is typeset.
- **No `.bbl` or `.bib` needed**: the references are a literal `thebibliography` in the master
  file, which is also the arrangement arXiv recommends for automated reference extraction.
- **No build product in the set.** The tarball is written *before* anything is compiled in the
  directory, so it cannot pick up a `.aux`, `.log` or `.pdf`.
- `figures/` holds **19** PDFs and the paper includes **11**; `arxivset.sh` copies only the 11, and
  arXiv asks explicitly that unused figures be left out. (The twelfth name a grep finds,
  `figures/appm_power.pdf`, occurs only inside a comment in `sections/app_N_rfi.tex` and is
  therefore in neither the PDF nor the set.)

## 5. The abstract in plain ASCII, for the arXiv metadata field

**1860 characters, 295 words.** Paste verbatim. Regenerate with
`python3 ../../referee_r17/absascii_r17.py` rather than editing by hand — every number in it is a
macro and will move.

```
Above 30 GHz, narrowband transmitter searches have reached one spectral line toward some tens of stars. We uniformly search 405 fine-channel windows toward 61 nearby systems in the ALMA archive, spanning 47.7 GHz of union bandwidth in disjoint intervals, plus 1258 coarser ones without drift discrimination. Windows are filtered for an unresolved carrier drifting linearly in the stellar frame. Threshold crossings are tested against catalogued transitions at the star's velocity, then sought again in later blocks. Nine injections in ten are recovered at 1.5 x 10^15 W for the median system, for a carrier narrower than the instrumental channel drifting below our default ceiling of 12.0 nHz, 6 per cent of the generally recommended ceiling. The injections carry no atmospheric decorrelation, so these limits are optimistic by it. No confirmed carrier is found. Of 56 crossings, 18 are attributed and 38 are not; the four coarse-window ones come from one block that fails a quality criterion. None of the unattributed crossings recurred, including the two that lead their own control fields, while 13 attributed to circumstellar or foreground CO do recur. The repeats exclude a carrier whose stellar-frame frequency returns to within +/-0.21 km s^-1 of the first epoch's and radiates through both observations, not a persistent transmitter of any kind. For 7 of them the repeats are too shallow for even that test at the trigger amplitude. The remainder is consistent with chance, on a step from the control positions to the star measured from only 8 reserved events. A calibration residual could mimic a carrier in 9 windows and is measured to act in none. Line emission, not interference, produces every astrophysical false positive and the strongest crossings. What limits the search is inherited coverage, not depth: 23 systems have no confirmation epoch.
```

The transliteration is `±`→`+/-`, `×`→`x`, `−`/`–`→`-`, `’`→`'`, and the two flattened
superscripts restored as `10^15` and `km s^-1`. Both repairs are **asserted** in
`absascii_r17.py`, so a future build whose extractor behaves differently fails there rather than
publishing `1015`.
