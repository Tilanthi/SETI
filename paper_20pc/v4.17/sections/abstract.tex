\begin{abstract}
Searches for narrowband transmitters above 30\,GHz have covered one
spectral line toward some tens of nearby stars. Uniformly searched, \NEB{}
ALMA execution blocks give \NWinA{} fine-channel windows of
\ChanLoA--\ChanHiA\,kHz toward \NSysClassA{} systems, covering
\CvUnionA\,GHz of union bandwidth in \CovNIslands{} disjoint intervals;
\NWinB{} coarser windows carry no drift discrimination.
Windows are filtered for an unresolved carrier drifting linearly in the
stellar frame; crossings are tested against catalogued transitions at the
stellar velocity, unattributed ones sought again in later blocks. For a
carrier narrower than the instrumental channel, nine injections in ten return
at $\EirpNinetySysMedA$\,W for the median system. The default drift ceiling
is \DriftCeilLo\,nHz, \AccelSheikhPct{} per cent of the \citet{Sheikh2019}
range. Of the \EvNCrossRaw{} crossings, \MkAttr{} are attributed and
\MkUnattr{} are not; \PrFlagXrossWord{} are in one
block failing a quality criterion applied after inspection. None of the
unattributed crossings recurred, including \PrRankClause, though one of them
is untested, while \MkAttrRecur{} attributed to
circumstellar or foreground carbon monoxide do recur. That excludes a carrier
holding its stellar-frame
frequency to within $\pm\RqDvMed$\,km\,s$^{-1}$, radiating through both
observations, not a persistent transmitter of any kind; the remainder is
consistent with chance to the control positions' accuracy. A per-cent-level
calibration residual could mimic a carrier in \CxNAdmit{} of the windows;
none holds a crossing. Line
confusion costs \MkMaskAPct{} per cent of the coverage and \RcNoRepSys{}
systems have no confirmation epoch; these, not sensitivity, limit the search.
\end{abstract}
