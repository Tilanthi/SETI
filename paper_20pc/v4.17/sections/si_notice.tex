\section*{Supporting Information}
The following appendices are published as online Supporting Information with
the electronic version of this paper. They are referred to by letter
throughout the text, and their sections, figures and tables keep the
numbering used here.

\begin{itemize}[nosep,leftmargin=1.2em]
\item \textbf{Appendix~A.} Spectral response, frequency conventions and the
  primary-beam response: the channel response of the correlator and what it
  does to an unresolved carrier, the three frequency frames and the
  transformations between them, the intra-integration smearing correction
  applied to every limit, and the primary-beam attenuation that turns a
  measured noise into a flux at the star.
\item \textbf{Appendix~B.} The spatial rank and what it can support: what the
  comparison with the \NCtrl{} control positions does and does not establish,
  the reserved out-of-sample hold-out that calibrates it, the channel-dependent
  calibration mechanisms and the measurement that excludes them, and the null
  of the recurrence statistic.
\item \textbf{Appendix~C.} The line mask: the velocity criterion, its
  evaluation in the star's own rest frame with the local standard of rest
  as a check, the
  published carbon-monoxide surveys each sightline is checked against, the
  masked bandwidth and what it costs, and the one species treated as a note
  rather than as an attribution.
\item \textbf{Appendix~D.} The false-alarm expectation and the control
  ensemble: what the expectation is conditioned on, the execution-block
  quality criterion, the calibration and the resolution of the control
  ensemble, the trials budget, and the crossing ledger itself.
\item \textbf{Appendix~E.} Radio-frequency interference screening.
\end{itemize}
