THE CLAIM THAT IS WITHDRAWN
Every version since v3.24 stated that a per-channel temporal-median subtraction suppresses
temporally persistent, frequency-stationary carriers, and that a classical continuous beacon
therefore lies outside what this survey constrains. That is wrong. The pipeline performs no
temporal-median subtraction. Its baseline step is a running median along FREQUENCY, 65
channels, applied per integration -- which by construction cannot remove a feature confined to
one or two channels, as its own docstring states -- and the de-drift step is an
inverse-variance-weighted sum over integrations, which accumulates a persistent carrier
coherently. The withdrawal is stated explicitly in the abstract, the introduction, the boxed
reading rule, the limitations box, the injection appendix and the future-work list.

MEASURED, TWO INDEPENDENT WAYS
Dwell-fraction campaign: 3,888 trial-level injections across 12 real windows (six coarse, six
fine, eleven targets), amplitudes 2-20 sigma per integration, dwell fractions 0.10-1.00, six
realisations each. Recovery rises monotonically with dwell. Pooling amplitudes at or above 4
sigma: coarse windows recover 100 per cent of continuous carriers, 100 per cent at dwell 0.25
and 83 per cent at 0.10; fine windows give 100, 91 and 71 per cent.

End-to-end through the unmodified released pipeline: a zero-drift, always-on tone injected into
the calibrated visibilities of a coarse Band 7 window at 1, 3 and 10 times the per-integration
rms returns T_star = 20.5, 65.3 and 217.7, recovered within one channel of the injected
frequency and flagged as a detection in every case. For 639 integrations the ideal coherent
gain is sqrt(639) = 25, so 20.5 at the per-integration noise level is essentially the
theoretical maximum. Nothing is being suppressed.

WHERE THE ORIGINAL "0 OF 500" CAME FROM
That campaign's frozen recovery criterion required the recovered peak drift to lie within one
grid step of the injected drift. On a coarse window every in-grid drift is sub-channel over the
track, so the trial grid is degenerate and the reported peak drift is arbitrary; the clause
fails even for a 200-sigma detection. A criterion artefact, read for four versions as physical
suppression.

CONSEQUENCES
The completeness gap covering 73 per cent of the searched windows -- which both referee rounds
identified as the survey's single biggest weakness -- is now filled: the coarse-channel
majority has a measured C(A, f_dwell). The survey does constrain the classical continuous
narrowband beacon. The genuine coarse-window limitation is narrower and still real: within the
searched ceiling those windows carry no drift discrimination, though their amplitude
sensitivity is full. That now heads both the limitations box and the next-release priorities,
replacing the withdrawn item. Trial-level injection records ship with the release, which also
answers the referees' standing request for per-injection provenance.

OPERATIONAL NOTE
While scoping this campaign the live survey was found deadlocked: the disk governor had been
blocking the driver for hours at 149G free against a 150G floor, because the target being
processed had accumulated 175G of per-spw split measurement sets in its own products directory.
The search step reclaims its waterfall intermediate but never reclaimed the split MS. Freeing
one completed spw's set unblocked the driver immediately, and the reclaim gap is now fixed in
seti_drift_search_generic.py so it cannot recur. No data were downloaded for the end-to-end
test: it reused a calibrated 2.2 GB coarse-window MS already on disk, and the test directory
was reclaimed afterwards.
