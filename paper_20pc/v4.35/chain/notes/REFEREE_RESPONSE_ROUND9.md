**A2 — completeness transfer. Option (ii) chosen** (option (i) is the

**A3 — the coarse "0 of 500" walk-back.** The account is now

**A4 — Results-opening contingency statement.** The Results section now

**A5 — CP−72 cross-match: run or justify concretely.** Justified
concretely, in the subsection: the catalogue half of a Gaia/2MASS/WISE
cross-match could list sources in the synthesised beam, but **without
the visibility-domain localisation it cannot say which, if any, the
excess belongs to** — a null match would not retire the flag and a
populated match could not promote it — so the check is scheduled with
that localisation rather than run as a catalogue query that cannot move
the disposition. We accept the referee's framing that a null result
would be one sentence of information; our judgment is that the sentence
would be uninterpretable until the position is fitted, and saying so
explicitly is the more useful disclosure than an uninterpretable null.

**A6 — closure-phase appendix compressed.** Appendix E is now a single
