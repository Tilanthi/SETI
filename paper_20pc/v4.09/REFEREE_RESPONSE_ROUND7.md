# Referee response — Round 7 (v3.36 → v3.37)

Response to both round-7 referee reports. Every change below is in
`technosignatures_20pc_v3.37.tex` (and the two regenerated figures) in this
folder. **Page parity held: 38 pages before and after**, 0 errors, 0 undefined
references, 0 overfull boxes. Where the referees offered options we chose one;
where a request needs data or compute the release does not carry (new

and point to the pre-registered commitment. No material has been moved to

## Referee Report 1

**1. Primary result ambiguity (sensitivity + zero candidates primary; population
a worked example).** Adopted. The Abstract now leads with sample/coverage, the
two-class search morphology, zero credible candidates, and the trigger-threshold
range with one physical benchmark; the Conclusions state the transmitter-fraction
machinery "demonstrates how occurrence constraints will be derived once
configuration-dependent completeness is measured; it is not quoted as a result
here."

**2. Remove ≈6% from Conclusions as a headline; label Fig. 11 / Tables 14–15 as
methodological demonstrations.** Adopted. The Conclusions no longer quote the
6.2% number as a constraint (it survives only inside App Q's demonstration
tables, which carry "a methodological demonstration for the completed survey,
not a prevalence measurement" in both captions and in the Fig. 11 caption:
"illustrative, not a prevalence bound"). §6.2 in the main text is now a
condensed statement ending at a pointer to the new App Q.

**3. Stratified injection campaign.** Declined for this release, with reason:
new injections into calibrated visibilities need archive pulls and cluster
reduction that the frozen release does not carry. The full stratified design
(bands, on-source time, channel count, beam offset, central/off-axis,
compact/extended) is pre-registered in §6.3 as the completed survey's
completeness design, including measurement of C(P, ν̇, f_dwell) per
configuration rather than a pooled number.

**4. End-to-end zero/near-zero drift injection for the fine class.** Declined
for this release (same reason as item 3). The unmeasured cell is now stated as
such rather than blurred: the completeness-surface figure caption identifies
zero through-pipeline recovery for f_drift ≤ 0.25 as "a measured property of
the through-pipeline path, distinct from the coarse-window criterion artefact",
post-baseline injection recovers near-static carriers, and Table 6 (class
completeness) marks the cell "post-baseline only". The end-to-end campaign is
pre-registered in §6.3.

**5. Rework Fig. 4 / demote the historical failed-recovery result.** Adopted.
The main-text presentation is now the corrected completeness surface; the
erroneous-criterion history (the 0/500 coarse return, the 20–200σ catch, the
withdrawn "suppression" reading) lives in App D and the development-audit
appendix, with the main text carrying one compressed paragraph that states the
withdrawal and links the audit tables.

**6. Class A/B boundary via a physical criterion (η_drift).** Adopted — the
referee's own suggested parameter. §4 now defines the classes by
η_drift = |ν̇_max| T / Δν_ch (computed per window; macros generated by
`round7_calc.py`), reports the per-class ranges, and states the residual edge
cases quantitatively (only N coarse windows reach η_drift ≥ 1, maximum value
given; N short fine windows sit below 1). The <5 MHz vs ≤1 MHz wording
inconsistency is gone.

**7. Coarse search = "unresolved spectral excess search".** Adopted. Class B
is named "the unresolved spectral excess search" at definition and in the
Abstract/glossary; "narrowband" is explicitly reserved for the assumed signal
morphology, not the resolving power (§1).

**8. Fig. 1 EIRP-comparison danger.** Adopted via annotation plus conversion
text rather than a two-panel split (page parity): the figure and its caption
state that vertical position is not technosignature merit and that the
competitive EIRP numbers arise from the d² factor; §4.2 gives the commensurability
convention (total power per channelisation; dividing by Δν_ch converts to
spectral power density) inline wherever the two appear together, and the ~4000×
Hz-channelisation price is quantified for the deepest window.

**9. Arecibo-radar label.** Adopted. Fig. 9 now carries "Arecibo-like radar:
power scale only, not a matched bandwidth or transmitter" on the plot itself,
and the Abstract's benchmark sentence carries "(a power scale, not a matched
transmitter model)". The explicit numerical example requested — how a genuinely
narrow radar-like signal populates an ALMA native channel — is the
1.0×10⁶ W Hz⁻¹ minimum detectable spectral power / 4×10⁹ W at 1 Hz passage in
App C.

**10. Primary-beam exchangeability settled now.** Adopted analytically. §5.2
now computes the beam FWHM per band (1.13λ/D, 12 m), shows the identical-radius
control ring has zero circular-beam differential by construction, bounds the
residual at the median stellar offset (0.02 per cent), gives the worst retained
offset (Sirius B) gain gradient, and constructs controls at equal gain along
ring azimuth under the holography model. The empirical stellar-rank
distribution under the null is the round-6 pseudo-target calibration (0.20
expected flags vs 0.84 rank budget; held-out 0.17, KS 0.73), which is unchanged
and remains the operative validation.

**11. 512-control floor = screening device; rename.** Adopted. The term is now
"stage-1 statistical flag" everywhere (Abstract, Results, glossary, crosswalk);
"candidate" is reserved for a flag surviving every veto, and the old name
survives only in the glossary crosswalk.

**12. Enlarged CP−72 controls now.** Declined, with reason stated in the paper
itself: new control positions need visibility-level re-extraction, which the
frozen products do not carry; the promote/retire criteria are pinned with the
explicit threshold (p < 1.2×10⁻⁴, of order 10⁴ positions needed) and the repeat
observation at 345.1152 GHz identified as the faster arbiter. The disposition
does not rest on the rank floor: it is reported as unclassified and
statistically non-significant.

**13. One prespecified family-level false-alarm procedure.** Adopted. The
Monte-Carlo pseudo-target calibration is the prespecified primary; Bonferroni,
Wilson, Poisson and block permutation are explicitly robustness checks against
it (App O summary table marks them as such; App Q states the rule and the
series-level BH plan).

**14. Mask as search-space exclusion; dev/held-out split explicit.** Adopted.
The mask is introduced as excluded search space (never searched-and-empty); the
§1 boxed Development/confirmation note states statistic-and-mask-defined-on-this-release
→ descriptive only, frozen → confirmatory; the flagged-windows table caption
repeats the developmental-vs-confirmatory distinction for the ±50 km s⁻¹ width.

**15. Eight species insufficient as a general veto.** Adopted as policy. The
paper now states the eight-species mask is the automated first-pass screen
only, and pre-registers scoring future out-of-mask crossings against a
Splatalogue/CDMS/JPL query of all catalogued transitions with an
astrophysical-line probability from source and environment — "the procedure
CP−72 2713 would have needed had it fallen in a line-rich field".

**16. Equation-level description of spectrum extraction.** Adopted. §4.1 now
specifies: per-integration, per-native-channel extraction on the delivered QA2
measurement set with archive flagging carried through untouched and no spectral
regridding at any stage; Stokes I from parallel hands at the propagated
position; baseline removal after extraction; primary-beam correction entering
the quoted thresholds rather than the ranked amplitudes; the visibility-domain
weighting fixed in the released code as the reproducibility reference, with the
per-window quantities itemised in Data Availability.

**17. Running-median transfer function.** Adopted analytically. App C's new
"Linewidth convention, and the running-median transfer function" subsection
gives the flat-below-W/2, zero-above-W behaviour, names W the linewidth
ceiling, and states the survey constrains channel-confined morphologies.

**18. Maximum transmitter linewidth / N_ch sensitivity function.** Adopted.
The same subsection derives EIRP_5σ(N_ch) ≃ N_ch × EIRP_5σ(1) for
N_ch ≲ W/2 with the sub-channel placement systematic (0.5–1 amplitude, mean
0.75) and the two-significant-figure quoting convention it motivates.

**19. Drift-grid mismatch losses.** Adopted. §4 reports the grid spacing as the
worst half-step residual in channels per class (macros from `round7_calc.py`),
and §6.3 pre-registers injections at continuously randomised drift rates and
phases rather than on grid points.

**20. Acceleration ceiling = computational boundary, not physical prior.**
Adopted. The text states "search definition, not a physical prior"; Table 3's
footnote quantifies the eccentricity amplification (1.4× at e=0.1, 1.9× at
e=0.2) so borderline planets are flagged; and Fig. (drift_acceleration) already
plots the selection function — maximum line-of-sight acceleration against
orbital period with the searched ceiling as a shaded band — which is the
requested sensitivity-versus-acceleration presentation.

**21. Linear-drift assumption / sinusoidal injections.** Declined for this
release (same compute constraint as item 3); the analytic boundary is in the
text (curvature matters when ½ν̈T² exceeds half a channel: P ≲ 1.6 d
fine-channel, ~1 h coarse), and sinusoidal-track injections following
representative sample orbits are pre-registered in §6.3.

**22. Epoch activity vs dwell fraction.** Adopted in text: the Fig. 11 caption
and both demonstration-table captions now define p_epoch against f_dwell
explicitly ("independent activity across a system's execution blocks, not the
intra-track dwell fraction"), §5.4's tail converts the epoch structure into the
constraint statement, and App Q's ladder paragraph states the phase-averaged
nature of the bound. A schematic figure was not added (page parity; the two
tables carry the distinction).

**23. Continuum analysis upgraded or shortened.** Shortened and demoted: the
continuum lane is Appendix K, §5.5 summarises it in compressed form, §1 states
"only the continuum screen enters dispositions in this release", and its
unquantified statistical power is stated, not claimed around.

**24. Closure phase / chirp / occupancy as demonstrations.** Adopted. §1:
"the other three are demonstrations of capability for deeper releases";
Conclusions repeat "demonstrated but with no discriminating power at these
flux densities"; the three appendices were compressed (closure phase 42→33

**25. Visibility-domain likelihood now.** Deferred with reason. There is no

**26. "Archive-conditioned census" wording.** Adopted: "ALMA-archive-conditioned

**27. Nearest-first selection effect on population plots.** Adopted. Both

**28. System as statistical unit; machine-readable system_id.** Adopted. The

**29. EIRP uncertainty budget.** Adopted. §4.2 now carries the systematic
